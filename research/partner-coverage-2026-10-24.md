## Research method and evidence quality

New reports use official company pages and documentation for product facts. The S-Pankki Open Banking page, MariaDB vector reference, Intel OpenVINO overview, Context.dev docs, Featherless docs, Wendy docs, Aiven PostgreSQL docs and Oxylabs developer portal provide concrete developer entry points. Reports link their sources inline.

Targeted searches for the new challenge partners did not establish a published 2026 challenge brief. The [official participant portal](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026) and event page still describe tracks as forthcoming. We therefore provide proposed projects and mentor questions, not fabricated briefs.

Scailab's indexed official descriptions establish the general domain, but the main page did not expose readable detail; direct retrieval also failed. Founders House's page exposed only a title. These limitations are explicit in their coverage. No third-party company's details were substituted.

Company marketing metrics are not used as measured prototype performance. Pricing, quotas, credentials and event benefits are left unverified unless an actual cited source establishes them. No accounts were created, paid products purchased, APIs benchmarked or hardware used.

## What “complete” means here

Every organization in the dated official partner/supporter roster has research coverage. It does **not** mean all future challenge details have been published, all integrations have been tested or every older technical statement has been re-researched.

Recheck after brief release: new partners, mandatory integrations, datasets/licences, model or SDK changes, account entitlements, hardware availability, judging weights and submission rules. Update the relevant report rather than duplicating competing versions.

## Review checks

The change is documentation-only. Verify report/index coverage, all local Markdown links, unique filenames, presence of sources and evidence caveats, and preservation of prior reports. Review the PR file list and merge the reviewed head. No application test suite is applicable to this repository change.
