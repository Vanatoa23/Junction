# e16: Operations Board

## 1. Concept
A departures board for your supply chain: every supplier is a flight that is on time, delayed or cancelled. Calm and factual, readable from across a room, so "are we affected?" is answered in two seconds.

## 2. Navigation and screens
Three screens, one persistent status strip. **Board** (default), **Suppliers**, and **Add supplier** (a side sheet, not a page). The strip reads "12 suppliers watched · 1 needs attention · Last checked 2 min ago". A discreet "Test notice" button sits at the far right for the presenter.

## 3. Add-supplier flow
The sheet opens with one field, **Supplier name**, and one button, **Add supplier**. Below it, a quiet line "Add more details (optional)" expands four groups: Where (website, country, city), What (what they supply, criticality as Low / Medium / High chips), Terms (yearly spend, lead time in days), Contact and notes. Each group shows how many fields are filled. A **Paste a list** tab accepts one name per line. After saving, a checklist shows "Looking into this supplier..." with found facts and "Could not find: lead time". Nothing blocks on optional data.

## 4. Map
A muted world outline as the base, with pale grey land and a navy hub square labelled with the company name. Direct suppliers are circles joined to the hub by thin grey lines, nothing deeper. Zoomed out, nearby markers cluster into a count badge, coloured by the worst status inside, and lines curve as arcs. Zoomed in, markers become small labelled cards with name and city, and lines run straight. Clicking a marker slides in a panel: name, risk level, reason, and a "See replacements" button. Colours: ready is blue, checking is dashed outline, needs your choice is amber, disrupted is red. A disruption turns the affected lines red, shows a port icon with the caption "Closed since 08:40", and the hub reads "2 suppliers affected".

## 5. Risk and explanation
Each supplier shows a three-segment bar with the word Low, Medium or High, and the number 0 to 100 in small grey type. Beneath, up to three plain reasons: "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." Every reason carries a source line: "From public news, 2 days ago". Where evidence is missing it says "Not enough information yet". It never guesses.

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
Alerts are departure-board rows: time, what happened ("Port of Rotterdam closed by strike"), where, affected suppliers, severity badge, and "Noticed 90 seconds after publication". Newest first, five visible, then "Show earlier". Opening a row lists three ranked replacements, each with a status word, **Safe to approve**, **Needs a check** or **Ruled out**, and one sentence of reason. Ruled-out options collapse by default. The approve moment is a single confirmation: "Switch Acme Metals to Lyon Steel? This records your decision." with **Approve** and **Cancel**. The row then reads "Replacement approved by you, 14:02".

## 7. Visual style
Light by default, with a dark top strip that echoes an airport board.
- Background #F4F6F8, surface #FFFFFF, ink #1B2430, muted text #5B6775
- Hub navy #1F3A5F, neutral blue #3E6E9E, lines #9AA7B5
- Amber #D9902B, high risk red #C2412D, safe green #2E7D5B
- Status strip #14202E with text #E9EEF3
Typography: Inter, 16px body, 14px labels, 28px for headline numbers, tabular numerals for times. Spacing on an 8px grid, 24px card padding, 16px side gutter. Mood: calm and precise. The only motion is one pulse when a new disruption arrives.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
Deeper supply tiers on the map, login, a settings page, trend charts, raw source lists beyond one link per reason, confidence modes, labels that appear only at continent zoom, decorative animation, and a dark mode toggle.
