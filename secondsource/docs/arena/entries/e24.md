# e24: Illustrated and human

## Concept
A calm, friendly helper that tells you in plain words when a supplier is in trouble and who can step in. Every supplier wears a friendly icon, so the map reads like a picture book, not a control room.

## Navigation and screens
Three screens, labelled "Suppliers", "Map" and "Alerts" in a rounded top bar. The app opens on Map with your company in the centre and one reassuring line above it: "All 10 suppliers are running normally" or "One supplier is affected: Port of Rotterdam." Alternatives open as a side sheet from an alert, never as a new screen.

## Add-supplier flow
One warm card: "Who do you buy from?" The name is the only required field, in a large input. Below it, soft chips offer optional extras: "Add website", "Add location", "Add what they supply", "Add more details". Each chip opens one small section and can be skipped. A "Paste a list" link accepts one name per line. After saving: "Thanks. We are looking into them now. Found 4 facts so far, couldn't find 1 yet."

## Map
A rounded, muted base map: sand-coloured land, pale blue sea. The hub is a large rounded house icon. Each direct supplier is a round badge with a category icon: ship (port or transport), factory (manufacturer), box (materials), leaf (food or packaging), shield (services). Lines are soft curves in teal, dashed when watched. Zoomed out, nearby badges merge into one bubble showing a count. Zoomed in, badges show the name and city. Clicking a badge opens a rounded card with the name, city, a plain status sentence and the risk level in words. A disruption appears as a soft coral circle over its place, labelled in words ("Port of Rotterdam: strike, started 2 hours ago"). Affected badges and lines turn coral and gently pulse once. The hub shows a short note: "Why: 2 of your suppliers ship through this port."

## Risk and explanation
Risk leads with a word, "Low", "Medium" or "High", on a rounded meter, with the number in small print. Reasons follow as short sentences with a small icon each: "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." A footnote reads "Based on public news and where its suppliers are." Never show a number without a sentence.

## Alerts and alternatives
Alerts are soft cards, newest first: icon, plain title, supplier names, "Noticed 3 minutes ago", and a pill reading "Serious" or "Watch". Alternatives are three ranked cards, each with a supplier icon, one reassuring sentence and a status pill: "Safe to approve" (teal), "Needs a check" (amber) or "Ruled out" (grey, not clickable). The approve button is large and fully rounded: "Yes, switch to this supplier." Afterwards: a check icon and "Done. Your choice is saved."

## Visual style
Light mode only. Palette: background #FBF8F3, surface #FFFFFF, ink #2B2D42, muted text #6B6F7B, healthy teal #2A9D8F, affected coral #E76F51, watch amber #E9C46A, ruled-out grey #B8BDC6, sea #DDEBF2. Typography: Nunito throughout, 16px body, 28px headings. Spacing on an 8px grid, 24px card padding, 20px card radius, pill buttons. Icons are two-tone line icons with rounded caps and a 2px stroke. Mood: warm, calm, reassuring.

## What you deliberately leave out
Dark mode, tables, charts beyond the risk meter, login, settings, deeper supplier tiers on the map, technical or provider names, and cartoon mascots that would distract from the message.
