# e10: Tablet and touch first

## Concept
A calm control board that one finger can run: large cards, large targets, nothing that needs a mouse. It should feel like a steady wall display that tells a non-expert in seconds whether anything is wrong.

## Navigation and screens
Three screens, named in plain words: **Overview** (default), **Suppliers**, **Add supplier**. A bottom bar holds three large labelled buttons, 72px tall. The presenter's "Live demo" control sits in the top-right corner and opens only after a long press, so nobody hits it by accident. Alerts and details open as bottom sheets over the current screen and never navigate away.

## Add-supplier flow
A bottom sheet, one question per view. Step 1: "Supplier name", the only required field, with a large input. Step 2: "What do they supply?" as large tappable chips. Then one row, "Add more details (optional)", with chips for Website, Country, City, Yearly spend, Lead time, Contact and Notes. Tapping a chip opens only that field. "Paste a list" accepts one name per line. On save, a card reads "Found 6 facts. 2 could not be checked", with each item tappable.

## Map
Full-width Leaflet map. Our company is a large dark hub in the centre, labelled "Your company". Each direct supplier is a 44px marker joined to the hub by a 3px grey line. Pinch and double-tap zoom both work. Zoomed out, nearby markers merge into numbered bubbles; zoomed in, they split into named pins. Tapping a marker opens a bottom sheet with name, status and risk. A disruption turns affected lines red and pulses affected markers once (the pulse is disabled when reduced motion is set). The hub shows a banner such as "Port of Rotterdam closure affects 3 suppliers".

## Risk and explanation
Each supplier shows one pill: Low (green), Medium (amber) or High (red), with its 0 to 100 number beside it. Below it, up to three reasons in full sentences, such as "Two of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." Tapping a reason highlights that place on the map. No charts, no decimals.

## Alerts and alternatives
The alert list is a stack of 96px cards: a plain headline, the place, the number of suppliers affected, and "noticed 2 minutes ago". Tapping one opens a sheet with the affected supplier and up to three alternative cards, each labelled "Safe to approve" (green), "Needs a check" (amber) or "Ruled out" (grey, not selectable), with one sentence of reason. The approve button is full width, 64px, reading "Approve this replacement". Approval shows "Approved and recorded", with Undo for five seconds.

## Visual style
Light and paper-like. Background #F6F7F5, surface #FFFFFF, ink #1C2320, secondary text #5B6661, lines #D9DEDB, hub #1F5F5B, safe #2E7D4F, check #B7791F, ruled out #8A9390, high risk #B42318. Type: system sans. Body 20px minimum, headings 32px, no label under 16px. Spacing on an 8px grid. Touch targets at least 48px, primary actions 64px. Dark mode is out of scope for the first pass.

## What I deliberately leave out
Hover states, tooltips, right-click menus, keyboard shortcuts, data tables, dense filters, login, settings, export, and deeper supply tiers on the map. Also any technical status words. The presenter's control is the only extra element.
