# e01: Map-first

## 1. Concept
The map is the whole app. Your company sits at the centre, suppliers around it, and trouble appears as a glow on the map before it appears as text. Everything else floats over the map as calm cards that never hide the hub for long.

## 2. Navigation and screens
One screen, full bleed. There is no menu bar and no login.
- Top left: **Suppliers** card (count, list, search-free).
- Top right: **Alerts** pill (count of open alerts). Opens a card list.
- Bottom centre: **Add supplier** button.
- Bottom right, small and grey: **Show a test notice** (presenter only).
- Overlays slide in from the right: supplier detail, alert detail, **Find replacement**.

Three overlay types, no pages. Plain names only.

## 3. Add-supplier flow
A bottom sheet, one visible field at a time after the name.
1. **Name** (only required field, large, autofocus).
2. **Where?** Country and city, one line. Website optional beneath.
3. **What do they supply?** One line.
4. **How critical?** Three-way control: Low, Medium, High. Default Medium.
5. A quiet link: **Add more details (optional)** expands spend, lead time, contact, notes.

**Paste a list** is a tab beside the form: one name per line, each becomes a row. Save closes the sheet with a line: "Checking the web for this supplier." Found and not-found facts appear later in supplier detail under two headings: "What we found" and "What we could not confirm."

## 4. Map
- **Hub:** dark navy dot labelled with the company name.
- **Lines:** thin, light grey, gently curved, from the hub to direct suppliers only.
- **Markers:** solid circles, size by criticality. Colours: ready teal, checking pale blue with slow pulse, needs your choice amber, could not find hollow grey.
- **Zoom out (international):** markers only, nearby ones merge into a count bubble.
- **Zoom in (city):** supplier names and city labels appear. Lines thicken.
- **Click a marker:** a small card beside it shows name, status in words, risk badge, and "Open".
- **Disruption:** a soft coral ring pulses over the incident. Affected lines turn coral. The map eases to fit the incident and affected suppliers. The hub shows one sentence: "Port of Rotterdam closed. 3 of your suppliers are affected."
- **Offline:** a plain pale grey world outline replaces tiles.

## 5. Risk and explanation
Each supplier shows one badge: **Low**, **Medium** or **High risk**. The 0 to 100 number is only in a tooltip. Under the badge, up to three plain reasons in full sentences: "Two of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." If facts are missing, say so: "We could not check 2 facts, so this may be higher."

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
- **Alert card:** headline in plain words ("Port of Rotterdam closed by strike"), affected suppliers, "Noticed 12 seconds after publication", severity word.
- **Find replacement:** three ranked cards, each with a state badge: "Safe to approve" (teal), "Needs a check" (amber), or "Ruled out" (grey, collapsed by default). One sentence reason under each name.
- **Approve moment:** one large button, "Use this supplier." It becomes "Approved today at 14:32" with a small Undo link. The decision is saved.

## 7. Visual style
- Light mode only.
- Palette: map land `#E9ECEF`, water `#DDE6EE`, surfaces `#FFFFFF` at 94 percent, ink `#1B2430`, secondary ink `#5B6675`, hub `#1F3A5F`, ready and safe `#2A9D8F`, checking `#8AA0B8`, needs choice and check `#E9A23B`, affected `#D9534F`, ruled out `#8A929C`.
- Type: Inter. Body 16px, card titles 20px, labels 13px.
- Spacing on an 8px grid, 16px gutter, 12px card radius, soft shadow.
- Mood: daylight, quiet, unhurried. One pulse animation only.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
Logins and settings. Dashboards, charts and tables. Drawing tier-two suppliers, ports or owners. Search and filters. Dark mode. Mobile layout. Provider names, job states and any technical status words. Animated transitions beyond the pulse. Export and reports.
