# e11: Head of Sales lens

## 1. Concept
Open on one question: which customer orders are at risk, and for how long. Every other part of the app exists to answer it calmly, like a ledger that quietly turns amber when an order is in danger.

## 2. Navigation and screens
Three screens with plain names: **Today** (opens first), **Suppliers**, **Map**. A grey "Show a sample problem" button sits far right in the top bar for the presenter. No menus, no login.

## 3. Add-supplier flow
One panel. The name field is the only required field and is focused on open. Below it, a line reads "Know more? Add what you have" and expands in place to: Website, Country, City, What they supply, How critical (Low / Medium / High pills), Yearly spend, Lead time in days, Contact, Notes. Empty fields are never shown as errors. A "Paste a list" tab takes one name per line. After saving, the card shows two short lists: "We found" (green) and "We could not check yet" (grey).

## 4. Map
A deep teal circle labelled "Your company" sits at the centre. Lines run only from the hub to direct suppliers, 1.5px in #9AA5A0. Markers are coloured by risk: low #2E7D5B, medium #B7791F, high #B4432F. Zoomed out, suppliers merge into one count bubble per country. Zoomed in, each marker shows its name and city. Clicking a marker opens a side card with status, risk level, reasons and the orders it feeds. During a disruption, the incident gets one ring at its location, affected lines turn red, and the hub card states: "Your company is affected through 3 suppliers. Port of Rotterdam is closed." Map tiles are desaturated so colour keeps its meaning.

## 5. Risk and explanation
Lead with the words, not the number. Each supplier shows "High risk" with a small 0 to 100 figure beside it, then up to three reasons as full sentences, such as "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." Each order on Today carries the same level as a coloured dot.

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
The Today screen opens with an alert banner: "Port of Rotterdam strike. Noticed 2 minutes ago. 3 suppliers, 4 orders at risk." Below it is a table with columns Order, Customer, Due, Supplier, At risk for. "At risk for" reads "about 9 days late if nothing changes", estimated from the notice's expected length minus days of stock on hand. Clicking an order opens Alternatives: three ranked cards, each labelled "Safe to approve" (green), "Needs a check" (amber) or "Ruled out" (grey, Approve disabled), with one sentence of reason. The approve moment is one button, "Approve this replacement". Then a line confirms "Approved by you at 14:32", and the affected orders update in place in the table.

## 7. Visual style
Light only. Background #F6F7F5, surface #FFFFFF, border #E1E5E2, text #1C2723, secondary text #5C6863, hub #1F4D45, ruled out #8A9099. Inter throughout: 16px body, 28px headings, 13px column labels. 8px spacing grid, 24px card padding, 12px radius, no shadows. The mood is a calm operations ledger, not a dashboard.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
No login, settings or theme switch. No deeper supplier tiers on the map. No order entry (demo orders are seeded). No dark mode, phone layout, charts, email or push alerts. No technical labels or confidence modes; the reasons carry the explanation.
