# SecondSource: Radical Minimalism

## 1. Concept
A near-empty page of black, white and grey, where red is the only colour and appears only when a supplier is in danger. Calm by default, impossible to miss when it matters.

## 2. Navigation and screens
Three screens: **Map** (opens first), **Alerts**, and **Suppliers** (a plain list). Details and the add form slide in from the right. The presenter's "Demo" link sits quietly in the footer.

## 3. Add-supplier flow
One field: "Supplier name", then Save. Below it, a quiet line: "Add more details (optional)". It reveals website, country, city, what they supply, criticality (Essential, Important, Nice to have), yearly spend, lead time, contact and notes. Every field may stay empty. "Paste a list" accepts one name per line.

## 4. Map
Light grey land, white sea. "Our company" is a black hub at the centre. Each direct supplier is a black dot on a thin grey line. Status is shown by shape: filled for ready, hollow for checking, ringed for needs your choice, dashed for could not find. Zoomed out, dots become a count per country. Zoomed in, names appear. A click opens the supplier panel. A disruption turns the affected dot and its line red and adds one red label at the incident. The hub caption explains: "3 suppliers affected by the Port of Rotterdam closure."

## 5. Risk and explanation
Lead with the word, then the number: "High risk, 78 of 100". Red appears only for high. Below, up to three full-sentence reasons, each with the age of the check: "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam. Checked 10 minutes ago." Gaps are listed too: "We could not check 1 of its suppliers."

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
Alerts sit in one column, newest first, each with a headline, place, number of suppliers affected, and "Noticed 4 minutes after publication." Replacements appear as up to three rows: name, country, one-sentence reason, and one tag: "Safe to approve" (black), "Needs a check" (outline) or "Ruled out" (grey, struck through). Approve is one black button. Afterwards the row reads "Approved by you, 14 Nov, 10:42" with an Undo link. No confirmation dialog.

## 7. Visual style
Light only. Background #FFFFFF, surface #F4F4F4, hairlines #D6D6D6, secondary text #6B6B6B, primary text #111111, danger #C62828 used for nothing else. Inter (or system sans): 16px body, 14px secondary, 24px headings at weight 500, tabular figures. 8px spacing grid, 24px margins, hairlines instead of cards or shadows. Mood: a quiet printed page.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
Dark mode, login, settings, notifications, charts, decorative icons, hover tooltips, extra colours, deeper supply tiers on the map, technical terms, map clustering, and animation beyond one pulse.
