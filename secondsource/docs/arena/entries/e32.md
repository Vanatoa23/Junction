## 1. Concept
A weather forecast for your supply chain. One glance tells you whether any supplier is under a storm and what to do about it. The feeling is calm confidence, like checking the forecast before leaving home.

## 2. Navigation and screens
Three tabs in plain words: **Today** (opens first), **Map**, **Suppliers**. Alerts open as a panel over Today; alternatives open from inside an alert. A discreet **Stage** button in the corner opens "Publish a test notice".

## 3. Add-supplier flow
One large field, "Supplier name", and an Add button. Below it, optional chips: Website, Where they are, What they supply, How important, Yearly spend, Delivery time, Contact, Notes. Each chip opens a small inline field; only the name is required. "Paste a list" accepts one name per line. Afterwards a card reads "We found 5 things. We could not find 2," with an "Add city" prompt for the gaps.

## 4. Map
Our company is a dark-teal hub in the centre. Each direct supplier is a dot on a thin grey line; nothing deeper is drawn. Zoomed out, nearby dots merge into a count ("4 suppliers"); zoomed in, names appear. A click opens a side card with status, risk level and reason. A disruption shows as a soft coral circle over its place (Port of Rotterdam); affected lines turn coral and the hub card explains why. Motion is slow; nothing flashes.

## 5. Risk and explanation
Show a level word first (Low, Medium, High) with a five-dot meter. The score stays hidden. Below, one or two plain sentences: "Medium. Two of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." Each reason names its source in words. Missing facts are stated plainly: "We could not check its fire record yet."

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
Alerts are a short list of cards: a plain headline ("Rotterdam port closed for 2 days"), "Affects 3 of your suppliers", "Noticed 4 minutes ago". Alternatives are three ranked cards with a badge: green "Safe to approve", amber "Needs a check", grey "Ruled out" (collapsed). Each has one sentence of reason. Approve is one large button; then the card reads "Approved by you, 09:14. We keep watching," with Undo for 10 seconds.

## 7. Visual style
Light only. Paper #F7F6F2, text #1F2A2E, hub and buttons #0F5C5C, safe #2E7D5B, check #B7791F, high risk #C2453A, ruled out #8A9296, map lines #B8C2C4. Inter; 16px body, 14px minimum, headings 28 and 20. 8px spacing grid, 12px card radius, 1px borders, no shadows.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
No login or settings. No deeper tiers, ports or plants on the map. No numeric score, charts or tables. No provider names, job states or technical labels. No dark mode, email alerts, filters or onboarding tour.
