## 1. Concept
Your supply chain as a calm bank statement: each supplier is a card, each risk one big, honest number. The feeling is quiet control, as if someone is watching the money for you.

## 2. Navigation and screens
Three tabs: **Today**, **Map**, **Suppliers**. Two more screens open from them: **Alert** and **Add supplier**. Today shows three big numbers (Suppliers, Need attention, Ready to approve), the live alert, then supplier cards. A small grey "Demo" pill in the bottom-right corner publishes a test notice.

## 3. Add-supplier flow
A side sheet. Name is the only required field and is enough to save. "Add more details" reveals nine chips (Website, Country, City, What they supply, How important, Yearly spend, Lead time, Contact, Notes). One tap opens one field; nothing is forced. "Paste a list" takes one name per line. Afterwards the card shows what was found and what could not be found.

## 4. Map
Muted grey basemap. Your company is a navy hub at the centre. Direct suppliers are white markers ringed by level: low #12A594, medium #E0A100, high #E5484D. Lines are thin grey and turn red when affected. Zoomed out, markers merge into numbered regional bubbles; zoomed in, they show names. A click opens a card with name, score, one reason and "See alternatives". A disruption adds a pulsing ring on the port and a banner: "Port of Rotterdam is closed. 3 suppliers affected."

## 5. Risk and explanation
A 72px score inside a thin ring, with "High risk" beneath. Two or three reasons as plain sentences: "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." Grey footer: "Based on 8 facts. Could not check ownership." Colour never stands alone; every colour has a word.

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
The Alert page opens with a plain headline and a fact row: "3 suppliers affected · Noticed 2 minutes after publication · High". Left: affected suppliers. Right: alternatives as stacked cards with a pill: "Safe to approve" (green), "Needs a check" (amber), or "Ruled out" (muted, collapsed). Each card has one reason sentence and a full-width "Approve" button. Approving turns the card into a receipt, "Approved today, 14:05", with Undo for 10 seconds.

## 7. Visual style
Light only. Background #F4F6FA, surfaces #FFFFFF, hairlines #E4E8F0, ink #0E1726, secondary text #5B6678, primary #2F5BEA, safe #12A594, check #E0A100, risk #E5484D, ruled out #8A94A6. Inter throughout: big numbers 72px semibold with tabular figures, headings 24px semibold, body 16px. 8px spacing grid, cards with 24px padding, 20px radius, shadow 0 1px 2px rgba(14,23,38,.06) plus 0 8px 24px rgba(14,23,38,.08). Airy, with one accent per screen.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
Dark mode, login, settings, history charts, dense tables, deeper tiers on the map, filters, email or push alerts, product tours, and any technical term. How facts were found stays behind one "How we checked" link.
