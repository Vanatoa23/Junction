# Proposal e04: Warm and friendly

## Concept
SecondSource feels like a caring neighbour who tells you plainly when a supplier is in trouble and quietly finds a backup. It is calm and reassuring, never an alarm.

## Navigation and screens
Three screens, named in plain words: **Home** (a friendly summary such as "Almost everything is running smoothly"), **Map** ("Where your suppliers are") and **Suppliers** (soft cards). Desktop uses a left bar with icons and words; tablet uses a bottom bar. Alerts sit at the top of Home. A small "Show an example" button runs the stage demo.

## Add-supplier flow
One card titled "Add a supplier". Only one field is required: "What's their name?" Beneath it, four soft chips: "Where are they?", "What do they supply?", "How important are they?", "Add more details". Each chip opens one short question at a time, and every answer can be skipped. "Paste a list" accepts one name per line. After saving, one line reads: "Thanks. We're looking into them now and will tell you what we find."

## Map
A pale, rounded map. Your company is a warm circle labelled "You" at the centre, with thin soft-grey lines to each direct supplier only. Markers: sage (steady), amber (worth watching), coral (affected). Zoomed out, nearby markers merge into counted bubbles; zoomed in, they open into names. Clicking a marker shows its name, one status line and a "See options" button. A disruption is a soft pulsing ring labelled in plain words, such as "Rotterdam port is closed", and the affected lines turn coral.

## Risk and explanation
Each supplier shows one friendly label: "Steady", "Worth watching" or "Needs attention". Beneath it sit two or three reasons written as full sentences, such as "Two of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." No numbers appear unless the user taps "More".

## Alerts and alternatives
Alerts are soft cards, newest first, with a plain title ("Strike at Rotterdam port"), the suppliers affected and "Spotted 4 minutes after it started." Tapping opens three option cards: "A good fit" (sage), "Let's double-check" (amber) and "Not a good fit" (grey, folded away), each with one sentence explaining why. Approval is one large button, "Yes, switch to this one", followed by "Done. We've saved your choice." No extra dialog.

## Visual style
Light mode only. Background #FBF8F3 (warm cream), cards #FFFFFF, text #2E2A26, secondary text #7A716A, sage #5E8C6A, amber #E9B44C, coral #E07A5F, lines #E8E1D8, map land #F3EEE6, water #E4EEF0. Headings in Nunito, body in Inter, 16px base with 1.5 line height. Spacing on an 8px grid, 16px card radius, very soft shadows. Mood: sunny, unhurried, reassuring.

## What we deliberately leave out
Dark mode, login, charts, tables, scores out of 100, tier diagrams, deeper supply lines, settings screens and any technical wording. Animation is limited to one gentle pulse.
