# e03: Dark control room

## Concept
A calm dark control room where the map is the page and supply lines glow softly, like instrument traces. Only what needs attention gets light: trouble shows as a steady warm glow and one plain sentence, never flashing.

## Navigation and screens
Three screens, named in plain words: "Overview" (map and alerts, the default), "Suppliers" (the list, with an add button), and "Alternatives" (opened from an alert). A slim left rail carries text labels, not icons alone. The presenter control is a small grey "Run demo notice" button in the bottom-right corner, opening a one-line panel.

## Add-supplier flow
A panel slides in from the right with the name field focused. Order: Name (required), then Website, Country and City on single lines, then "What they supply". Below them, a quiet line reads "Optional: helps us check more facts", with a collapsed "Add more details" section for how critical it is (Low, Medium, High), yearly spend, lead time, contact and notes. Enter saves with the name alone. A "Paste a list" tab accepts one name per line. After saving, a short result reads "Found 4 facts. Could not check opening hours."

## Map
Leaflet on a dark tile set; the offline fallback draws the world outline in #1E2B42. The company is a glowing hub at the centre, labelled "Our company". Each direct supplier is a small dot joined to the hub by a thin curved line (1.5px, #38D5FF at 60 percent). Zoomed out, nearby dots merge into a count. Zoomed in to city level, dots show names. Clicking a dot opens a card with the name, status, risk level and reason sentence. A disruption turns the affected lines amber and makes them breathe slowly (2 seconds, opacity 0.5 to 1), and the hub label states the cause: "Port of Rotterdam closed. 3 suppliers affected." Deeper tiers are never drawn.

## Risk and explanation
The score appears as a horizontal bar with a word beside it: "Medium, 58". Beneath it, at most three reasons as full sentences, such as "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." A small "Why?" link expands the source and date. No other numbers are shown.

## Alerts and alternatives
The Overview alert list has one card per incident: "Port of Rotterdam is closed by a strike. 3 of your suppliers are affected. Noticed 4 minutes ago." Severity is a word, never colour alone. Opening it shows up to four ranked replacements, each with a pill: "Safe to approve" (green), "Needs a check" (amber) or "Ruled out" (grey, with the reason shown). Each row has a name, city and one-sentence reason. "Approve this replacement" is the only filled button on screen. Approving records the decision inline with "Recorded today at 14:05 by you", with no modal.

## Visual style
Dark only. Background #0B1220, panels #121C2E, borders #22314A, text #E6EDF7, secondary text #93A4BD, hub and lines #38D5FF, safe #3DDC97, medium risk #F5B942, high risk #FF5C6C, ruled out #5B6B82. Font Inter: headings 24/32 semibold, body 16/24, labels 14/20 at minimum. Spacing on an 8px grid, 24px panel padding. Glow applies only to the hub and lines (12px soft shadow at 40 percent of the line colour). Motion stays under 2 seconds and is switched off under reduced-motion.

## What we deliberately leave out
Login, deeper-tier lines or lists, charts and dashboards, notifications, exports, a settings screen, sound, map flight animations, and any technical or provider names. The map carries one caption line only: "Lines show direct suppliers. Colour shows risk."
