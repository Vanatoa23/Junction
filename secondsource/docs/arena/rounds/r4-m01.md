# Round 4, match 01: e08 vs e09

**Winner: e09** (the five-beat incident story on a Today screen)

## Why

e09 answers the Head of Sales' question in one line: "Port of Rotterdam is closed. 3 of your suppliers are affected." The five timed beats (we noticed, we checked, suppliers affected, replacements found, you approved) also answer "since when" and "how fast" without numbers, which suits non-technical readers and the calm, editorial brief. e08 is well made and its map explains itself better at zoom, but its score-first card and three-screen layout ask the reader to decode more before they understand the situation.

## Two ideas from e08 worth stealing

1. **Zoom-level rollups that explain themselves:** at continent zoom, each country shows its count and highest-risk colour; at city zoom, each circle carries its one-line reason. This is stronger than e09's plain merge into numbered clusters.
2. **"What we found / We could not find" panel:** each fact is shown with its source, and each gap gets one retry link. This makes the add-supplier search honest about what the system did not learn.

Ideas from e09 that e08 lacks and should be kept in the merge: the headline sentence on the first screen, and the offline grey world outline fallback that the brief asks for.
