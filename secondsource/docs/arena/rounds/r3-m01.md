# Round 3, match 01: e01 vs e08

## Winner: e08 (Risk score is the hero)

e08 wins because its map carries the product's core question on its own: every supplier is a circle coloured and sized by risk, with a one-line reason beside it, so a Head of Sales can see where the trouble is and what it is without opening anything. Its zoom behaviour is the stronger of the two, with country totals showing the count and the worst colour when zoomed out and names plus reasons when zoomed in, which meets the "explains itself at every zoom level" bar more directly than e01's status markers and count bubbles. Its add flow is also the simpler one, with a single name field, Enter to add, and all extras behind one link, and it stays buildable in a day. e01 is calmer and has the simpler screen count, but its map shows status rather than risk, and it reuses amber for both "needs your choice" and "needs a check", which blurs the meaning for a non-technical reader.

## Ideas worth stealing from e01 (the loser)

1. **Undo on the approval moment.** After approval, e01 shows "Approved today at 14:32" with a small Undo link. This is more honest and reversible than e08's "Approved 14 Nov. Recorded." with the button simply disappearing.
2. **Uncertainty tied to the risk level.** e01 says "We could not check 2 facts, so this may be higher" under the badge. Putting this sentence next to the risk level in e08's card would make the score's limits explicit without adding a technical term.
