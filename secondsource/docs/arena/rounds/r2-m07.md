# Round 2, match m07: e25 vs e28

**Winner: e25 ("The map is the beauty")**

e25 wins because it is the calmer, more honest design that a non-technical Head of Supply can read in seconds: colour appears only where risk is, the hub and direct-supplier lines are explained on the map itself, and the alerts and alternatives say plainly what was noticed, when, and why a replacement is ruled out. e28 has a strong demo moment, but its breathing hub ring, dashed flowing lines, arcs and muted ruled-out cards add motion and hidden reasoning that work against "calm" and "honest" and cost more to build in one day. Both specs are clear on the add-supplier flow, but e25's name-first panel with quiet chips is slightly simpler to scan.

**Best idea from the loser worth stealing:** e28's "What we found" block after saving, which lists the facts found and states plainly what could not be found (for example, "Could not find yearly spend"). It answers the brief's "shows what it found and what it could not" more directly than e25's single follow-up line, and e25 could adopt it as-is.
