# Round 2, judge m08: e29 vs e32

**Winner: e32**

e32 is the stronger brief for a non-technical Head of Supply or Sales: the "weather forecast" concept and the Today / Map / Suppliers tabs are understood in seconds, and the score is replaced by a plain level word with a five-dot meter. Its add-supplier flow (one name field, optional chips that open small inline fields, "We found 5 things. We could not find 2") is cleaner than e29's collapsed block, and its alerts and alternatives are honest and short (Undo for 10 seconds, "We keep watching", ruled-out options collapsed). It is also within the 450-word limit and avoids e29's "lines follow real roads" requirement, which is not buildable in one day with Leaflet. e29 has the better map spec, but e32 wins on the higher-priority criteria and on feasibility.

**Idea worth stealing from e29:** affected suppliers never cluster. They always show as individual pins with a slow pulse, so an alert stays visible even at world zoom. e32's map merges dots into counts with no such rule, which could hide the disruption when zoomed out.
