# Round 3, match 03 verdict

**Winner: e21 (Zero-config demo mode)**

e21 wins because it does better on the criteria in priority order that matter most for this brief. Its add-supplier flow is the stronger one: Enter saves the name, and optional website, city and spend are quiet chips that expand in place, with honest results such as "Could not find a city. Add one?". Its alerts and alternatives are more honest, since ruled-out options stay visible as grey cards rather than hidden, and the approve moment has a ten-second Undo. Its palette and paper-like tone are calmer for a stage screen than e18's traffic-light red, amber and green, which reads as alarm before it reads as information.

**Two ideas from e18 worth stealing:**
1. A plain hub caption that names the incident and its impact on the map itself ("Port of Rotterdam closed. 3 suppliers affected."). e21 changes colours and adds a ring but never states in words why the hub is amber, which weakens criterion 3.
2. A pale world outline as the offline fallback, not only Europe. The brief asks for a world outline, and this keeps the zoomed-out international view working without tiles.
