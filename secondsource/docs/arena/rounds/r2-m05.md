# Round 2, match m05: verdict

**Winner: e18 (Traffic-light simplicity)**

**Why:** e18 passes the first test fastest: the home line "Two suppliers are affected by the Rotterdam closure" and a green, amber or red dot per supplier need no learning, and every dot carries its word, so colour never stands alone. Its map is pale and quiet with country counts zoomed out, names zoomed in, and a hub caption that explains the disruption, which covers the map criterion. It is also the easier build in one day, with three screens and no multi-pane layout to keep in sync with the map.

**Loser (e19) and its best idea to steal:** e19 is a well-considered control room with an honest approval step, and its best idea is the "Undo" link after "Approved, 14 Nov, 10:42" plus the rule that "Nothing changes without that click." Its three-column layout (list, map, drawer) is denser than a non-technical reader needs in seconds, so it loses on criterion 1.
