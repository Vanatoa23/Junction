# Round 1, match 12: e23 vs e24

## Winner: e24 (Illustrated and human)

e24 wins on the first two criteria, which the brief ranks highest. It opens on the map with one plain sentence ("One supplier is affected: Port of Rotterdam."), so a Head of Sales knows the state in seconds, and its warm rounded look with category icons reads as calm rather than as a trading desk. Its add-supplier card (name first, optional extras as chips that open one small section each) is the cleanest answer to "optional, never forced", and its hub note "Why: 2 of your suppliers ship through this port" explains the map at local zoom. Its weakness is real: it leaves out the presenter's "Test a disruption" control, which the brief requires, and its base map does not specify the offline outline fallback.

e23 is the more honest and more complete spec: it keeps reasons visible on ruled-out alternatives, shows "checked 14 Nov, 09:12" timestamps, names unknowns ("We could not check: ownership"), and includes the quiet presenter button. But its self-description, "trading-desk calm" and "quiet market terminal", with a dense 13px table and a status column, asks a non-technical user to parse a terminal, which fails criterion 1.

## Best idea of the loser worth stealing (e23)

Keep reasons visible on ruled-out alternatives and add "We could not check: ownership" as its own line under the risk reasons. Both make the app honest without adding technical words, and e24 can add them to its cards and add e23's quiet "Test a disruption" button to close its brief gap.
