# Round r1-m10 verdict

**Winner: e19** (three-pane control room: supplier list, map, slide-in drawer)

## Why

Both proposals are calm, legible, and buildable, so the decision turns on the map and the alert moment. e19 specifies the map more completely: clustering with counts when zoomed out, names when zoomed in, a labelled disruption circle, and the offline world-outline fallback that the brief explicitly requires, which e20 leaves out. Its approve step is also more honest, since it records the time and gives an Undo, while e20 has no way back once a supplier is chosen. e19's list grouped as "Needs your choice / Affected / Everything else" tells a non-technical person their state in seconds.

## Best idea from the loser (e20) worth stealing

Two persona chips on the inbox, "Affects materials" and "Affects deliveries", so Head of Supply and Head of Sales each see the same incidents framed for their concern. Adding this to e19 would fix its main gap, since it currently never addresses the two different users. The web-address autofill for country and city in the add sheet is a close second.
