# Round r1-m03 verdict

**Winner: e05** (five-question setup where the map draws itself)
**Loser: e06** (spreadsheet-familiar)

## Why e05 wins

The five-question setup is the fastest path to comprehension for a non-technical Head of Supply or Sales: one question per screen, a visible Skip, and a map that shows the company at the centre with each supplier line appearing as it is named. The look is calmer and more polished than e06 (no grid, no row chrome, a single light palette with no shadows), and the map explanation is strong at both zoom levels with country bubbles zoomed out, names zoomed in, and a hub banner that states the cause of a disruption. e06 is strong on honest alert and approval wording, but its four-column sheet with hidden toggles and a split screen is denser and less beautiful than e05's conversation-led flow.

## Best idea from the loser to steal

From e06: alerts state "Affecting you since 14 Nov, 09:10" on each alert, and an approved replacement keeps a visible record ("Approved by you, 14 Nov, 09:42"). Together these answer the Head of Sales question "since when" and make the approval decision auditable, both of which e05 leaves implicit.
