# Round 2, match m06: verdict

**Winner: e21 (Zero-config demo mode)**

**Why:** e21 is the simpler and more honest design. A non-technical reader gets "Today" and "Suppliers", one amber warning at Rotterdam, and a plain "Example data" line within seconds, and the calm paper palette fits the brief's "calm and polished" bar. Its alert and alternative rules are the strongest of the pair: each alert states when it was noticed, unchecked facts read "Not checked yet" instead of a guess, and approval has a 10-second Undo. e24 is warm and appealing, but its icon-per-category badges, rounded multi-colour styling and vaguer wording ("Found 4 facts so far") add more to read and do less to make the approval moment trustworthy.

**Best idea of the loser to steal (e24):** the disruption label on the map is written in words, for example "Port of Rotterdam: strike, started 2 hours ago", and the hub carries a one-line "Why" note ("2 of your suppliers ship through this port"). Adding these to e21's map would make the incident explain itself without opening a card.
