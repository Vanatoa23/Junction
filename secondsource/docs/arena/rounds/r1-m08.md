# Round 1, match 08: verdict

**Winner: e15 (Calm Bank Statement)**

Both proposals meet the brief's core needs, but e15 is the calmer and more buildable of the two. Its Today screen answers "are we affected?" in one glance with three big numbers, and its wording follows the brief's plain-language rules closely ("Safe to approve", "Needs a check", "Ruled out", "Based on 8 facts. Could not check ownership."). Its visual system is fully specified with hex values, type scale and shadows, and the light, airy look fits the stage-screen use better than e16's dark strip. e16 explains the map at zoom levels slightly better, but its departures-board metaphor ("on time, delayed, cancelled") does not map cleanly onto the real statuses and could confuse a non-technical reader.

**Best idea of the loser worth stealing (e16):** every reason on a risk score carries a source line such as "From public news, 2 days ago", and missing evidence says "Not enough information yet" instead of guessing. That makes the explanation honest in a way e15's footer alone does not, so add it to e15's reasons. A close second is e16's cluster badge coloured by the worst status inside it, which would make the zoomed-out map explain itself better.
