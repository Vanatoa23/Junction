# Round 1, match 11: verdict

**Winner: e21 (Zero-config demo mode).**

e21 wins because its map and alerts explain themselves at every zoom level and in every list, which is the brief's third and fifth criteria: zoomed out, nearby markers merge into per-country counts; zoomed in, names appear; alerts state what happened, what it touches, and how fast we noticed it. Its add-supplier flow is the fastest of the two (name, Enter, and optional chips that cost nothing to ignore), and its "Not checked yet" wording and 10-second undo are honest in the way the brief asks for. e22 has the better opening sentence and a calmer mood, but its four-screen add flow puts Country and City in front of the user as steps, which cuts against "optional, never forced", and its alerts show one at a time, so "how serious" and "how many" are harder to scan.

**Idea from the loser worth stealing:** e22's rule that the company's hub dot carries one plain sentence during a disruption ("3 suppliers are affected by the Rotterdam closure."). It explains the map's amber state in words at any zoom, so e21 could add it beside its existing amber ring.
