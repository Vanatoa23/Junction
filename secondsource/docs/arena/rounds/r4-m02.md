# Round 4, match m02: verdict

**Winner: e25 ("The map is the beauty")**

e25 puts the map first, which is what the brief asks for: the company is the hub, only direct supplier lines leave it, and zoom behaviour is spelled out (bubbles per country when zoomed out, names and cities when zoomed in). Its disruption treatment explains itself on the map itself: a soft red area over Rotterdam and a plain "Why: a port closure at Rotterdam affects 3 of your suppliers" line on the hub, so a non-technical reader sees where and why in seconds. Colour is reserved for risk (three risk colours plus the hub), which keeps the palette calm and the alerts honest, and the approve step records a decision without changing anything else. e21 is simpler to add to and has a nicer opening, but its disruption cue (an amber ring around the hub) says less about what is wrong and where, and it does not explain the map at local zoom as clearly.

**Ideas worth stealing from e21 (the loser):**
1. A ten-second "Undo" link after "Approve this replacement", so the approve moment is reversible and feels safe.
2. Inline per-supplier result lines ("Found: website, city" / "Could not find a city. Add one?") plus an offline pale outline of Europe in place of tiles, both of which cover brief requirements that e25 leaves unaddressed.
