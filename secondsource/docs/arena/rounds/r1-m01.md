# Round 1, match 01: verdict

**Winner: e01 (Map-first)**

**Why:** e01 makes the map the whole product, so a non-technical reader sees the company hub, direct suppliers and the coral incident ring in one glance, which matches criteria 1 and 2 with one pulse and calm cards. It is also the only proposal that specifies the map's behaviour at both zoom levels (merged count bubbles when zoomed out, names and thicker lines when zoomed in, direct-supplier lines only), which is criterion 3 as written. Its honest wording ("We could not check 2 facts, so this may be higher") and its add-supplier sheet (one field at a time, optional details behind a quiet link, paste-a-list tab) are as clear as e02's and feasible in a day.

**Loser's best idea to steal (e02):** the four status cards that answer "are we affected, how badly, since when" in one line (Suppliers affected, Highest risk, Deliveries at risk "since 14:20", Last checked). e01 should add a small status line to the top of the Alerts pill or the alert card so Head of Sales gets the "since when" answer without opening anything.

**Notes:** e02 is a sound, conventional dashboard, but its map gets 60% of a screen and gets less zoom detail. It also shows a 0 to 100 number and a bar on every supplier, which pulls against the brief's "plain-language reason" for non-experts. Both entries are within the 450-word limit.
