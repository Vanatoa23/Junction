# Round 1, judge m09: verdict

**Winner: e18 (Traffic-light simplicity)**

Why: e18 uses Low, Medium and High, always paired with their colour, which a Head of Sales reads in seconds with no metaphor to decode. Its map is the calmer and more readable of the two: a pale land and sea base, country counts when zoomed out, names when zoomed in, and a hub caption that states the impact in plain words. Its honesty rule is also stronger, since each card states what could not be checked, so silence never reads as safety. e17 is attractive and its weather language is charming, but "Advisory" and "Storm" ask the reader to translate the risk level first, and it hides the ruled-out options in the same list as the rest, which is less clear than the brief's three-way labelling.

Best idea of the loser worth stealing (e17): zoomed-out bubbles take the worst colour of the suppliers inside them, so a storm cannot hide inside a count. e18 should adopt this rule for its country counts.
