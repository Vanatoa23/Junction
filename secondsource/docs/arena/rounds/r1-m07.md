# Round 1, judge m07: e13 vs e14

**Winner: e13 (Radical Minimalism)**

e13 is the better design for this brief because it does what the brief asks in the simplest way: a short, calm list of alerts, a one-field add flow with everything else optional, and a single red signal reserved for danger. Its add flow ("Supplier name", then a quiet "Add more details (optional)") is the most usable of the two for a non-technical Head of Supply, and its one-black-button approve with Undo and no confirmation dialog fits the "approve is one click" requirement. It is also the easier of the two to build in one day, with one font, one red and no hatching or curved lines.

e14 is the more beautiful and more honest of the two. Its sourced reasons ("Harbour authority notice, 14 Nov") and its "What we found / Could not find" panel are stronger than e13's wording, and its colour-plus-square markers explain the map better than e13's shape-only status. However, e14 uses four status colours on the map, which dilutes "red only when in danger" and makes it a bit less calm, its alerts are full news stories rather than a short list, and its add form shows nine fields when the brief asks for almost nothing up front.

**Best idea from the loser to steal:** e14's source attribution on each reason, plus its "What we found / Could not find" summary after saving a supplier. Combine it with e13's approach by using colour and a square or dot shape together on the map instead of e13's hollow, ringed and dashed shapes, which are hard to read at a glance.
