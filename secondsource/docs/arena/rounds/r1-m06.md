# Round 1, match m06: e11 vs e12

**Winner: e12 (Head of Supply lens)**

e12 wins because it is the leaner design that fits the brief: one Today screen with one alert and one decision, a left rail of three items, and an add-supplier flow that saves on a single name and offers the optional fields as chips, so nothing overwhelms. Its alternatives are clear (three ranked rows, only the top safe option gets a filled Approve button, and the decision has an Undo), and it stays inside the brief's entities without inventing an orders database. e11 is a strong, calm design and its order-level "at risk for" line answers the Sales question "how badly" more directly, but it adds seeded orders, customers, due dates and a stock-on-hand estimate the brief never asked for, and its "about 9 days late" figure rests on data the app does not have, which weakens the honesty criterion and the one-day feasibility.

**Best idea of the loser to steal:** e11's per-order "at risk for" sentence ("about 9 days late if nothing changes"), which turns "how badly and since when" into one plain line for the Head of Sales. It should only be added if the expected disruption length is actually in the notice data, and labelled as an estimate.

**Criteria scoring (brief priority order)**
1. Understood in seconds: tie. Both open on a clear question and one alert. e12 is a touch quicker because it has one decision.
2. Calm, polished look: tie. Both are light, Inter-based and restrained.
3. Map explains itself: near tie. Both have a hub, direct-only lines, country count bubbles zoomed out, names zoomed in and a disruption ring. e11 colours markers by risk, which matches the score; e12 colours by status, which is slightly less aligned.
4. Add-supplier flow: e12. One required field, immediate save, optional chips. e11 covers more fields but expands them all at once.
5. Alerts and alternatives: e12, for its named supplier alert, single filled approve action and Undo. e11's quantified delay is the stronger idea but is less honest without the data behind it.
6. Feasibility in one day: e12, since e11 needs an orders model that the brief does not include.
