# Round 1, match 02: verdict

**Winner: e03 (Dark control room)**

e03 wins because its map explains itself at both zoom levels: a caption ("Lines show direct suppliers. Colour shows risk."), a hub label that states the cause ("Port of Rotterdam closed. 3 suppliers affected."), and affected lines that change colour, so a non-expert can read the picture without help. Its risk and alert wording is concrete and honest, with severity as a word, "Medium, 58" as a single score with at most three reasons, and "Ruled out" shown with its reason. The spec is also more buildable in a day, with exact tokens, glow rules limited to the hub and lines, and reduced-motion handling. e04 is warmer and easier to read at a glance, but its map carries no legend for its sage, amber and coral markers, and its disruption explanation sits only in a ring label.

**Best idea from the loser (e04) worth stealing:** the plain status words "Steady / Worth watching / Needs attention" as the primary risk label, with the number hidden behind "More". That is the easiest risk language for a Head of Sales to read, and e03 should use it in place of "Medium, 58" as the lead text.
