# Round 1, match 16 verdict

**Winner: e32** (weather-forecast concept: Today, Map, Suppliers)

**Why:** e32 can be understood in seconds by a non-technical Head of Supply or Sales: three plain tabs, a "Today" screen that answers "are we affected?", and a risk level word with a five-dot meter instead of a numeric dial. e31 is also calm and well-considered, but it adds three screens' worth of machinery (a 240-degree dial, a 30-day chart, a five-part alert grid, a slide-over panel) that works against the brief's "fewer, clearer things". e32 is also cheaper to build in one day of React + Leaflet, and its alerts and alternatives copy ("We keep watching", the 10-second undo on approval) is honest about what the system does.

**Best idea of the loser worth stealing (e31):** the add-supplier flow's single "Add more details (optional)" link, which hides criticality, spend, lead time, contact and notes behind one reveal. e32's row of eight optional chips is the weakest part of its proposal and would be better as this single progressive-disclosure link.
