# Entrant 34: Stratum, discovery-first (lens: Discovery agent excellence)

## 0. Bold bet
Most teams fake discovery with a canned list; we make it the most visibly real part of the product. Every inferred upstream node arrives with verbatim quotes, source URLs, and a confidence number calibrated on hand-labelled cases, which earns the Context.dev live-data points and gives Valio's controls judges a traceable reason for every edge.

## 1. Summary and name
Stratum: a discovery agent finds upstream producers from live web and news via Exa and Context.dev, scores each inferred edge with calibrated confidence and cited evidence, and feeds the hotel's risk view. Ordering stays minimal and human-gated.

## 2. Architecture and DB choice
- **Postgres 16 on Aiven** as the only store (Aiven bounty). Recursive CTEs cover traversal at about 200 nodes. ArcadeDB is rejected: its vector and traversal features are not needed at this size, and its deployment adds risk to the discovery path.
- **FastAPI service** plus a discovery worker polling a `job` table. No Kafka, no Valkey.
- **External:** Exa (Search, Contents, Websets, Monitors), Context.dev (enrichment, structured extraction, news, monitors with webhooks), Gemini (query generation and extraction).
- **Webhooks** reach the API through a tunnel (assumption). Progress streams to the page over Server-Sent Events. Cytoscape.js draws the graph.

## 3. Data model and key queries
- `company(id, name, domain UNIQUE, country, registered, industry, kind [seed, dummy, discovered])`
- `edge(id, seller_id, buyer_id, trust, confidence numeric(3,2) NULL, status [candidate, shown, rejected], run_id, UNIQUE(seller_id, buyer_id))`. Candidates below 0.5 stay `candidate` and are hidden from buyers.
- `evidence(id, edge_id, provider [exa, context], url, quote, source_type [partner, company_site, news, directory], domain, fetched_at, names_both_parties bool)`
- `discovery_run(id, target_id, query, status, budget_used jsonb, started_at, finished_at)`
- `calibration_bin(bin_lo, bin_hi, n_labelled, observed_precision)`
- `audit_log(id, actor, action, payload jsonb, created_at)`, append-only.

Trust is set by rule: discovery writes `inferred`, declaration `claimed`, both-side confirmation `agreed`, auditor stamp `audited`. Visibility is one SQL view per viewer role: buyers see their own map, and other companies only `shown` edges at `inferred` or higher. Seller-side lists are never exposed.

Key queries:
- **Upstream, depth 3:** `WITH RECURSIVE up AS (SELECT seller_id s, 1 d FROM edge WHERE buyer_id=$hotel AND status='shown' UNION ALL SELECT e.seller_id, up.d+1 FROM edge e JOIN up ON e.buyer_id=up.s WHERE up.d<3 AND e.status='shown') SELECT * FROM up;`
- **Hidden concentration:** upstream nodes with 2 or more distinct direct suppliers.
- **Blast radius of X:** recursive downstream walk from `seller_id=X`.
- **Alternatives:** opted-in sellers in the industry with a published sku price; min and max.

## 4. Agents (discovery, monitoring, procurement)
**Discovery** (core of this entry). Input: target company and a Gemini-generated query, for example "UHT milk ingredients supplier Finland". Budget per run: 20 Exa results, 8 Contents fetches, 6 enrichment calls, 4 extractions.
1. **Generate candidates:** Exa Search (category company, fast) for 20 results; Exa Search (category news, 12 months) for sourcing mentions. A Websets list ("EU dairy ingredient producers with sustainability reports") is pre-run Friday, since Websets is asynchronous.
2. **Resolve entities:** Context.dev enrichment gives canonical name, country and registration. Dedup on domain, then name plus country. No resolvable domain means rejection.
3. **Extract relations:** Exa Contents on news hits and supplier pages; Context.dev structured extraction with schema `{counterparties[], relation: supplies|buys_from|none, quote, quote_url}`.
4. **Verify in code:** the quote must contain both names (string match sets `names_both_parties`). Failures are logged as rejections with a reason.
5. **Score and write:** six-feature score (section 5), then `edge(status=candidate|shown)`.

Output: `{name, domain, country, relation, direction, confidence, evidence[{url, quote, source_type}]}`.

**Calibration.** Friday night, one teammate hand-labels 60 candidates against their sources. Logistic regression with 5-fold cross-validation. Reliability bins go in `calibration_bin` and show as "confidence 0.72, calibrated on 60 labelled cases".

**Monitoring.** Context.dev monitors on discovered supplier domains, plus an Exa Monitor on a news query. Webhooks trigger re-verification: corroboration raises confidence, retraction lowers it, each change logged. Recall, strike, insolvency and sanctions hits become `risk_signal` rows with a quote.

**Procurement.** Gemini drafts a switch from opted-in alternatives, citing the signal and price rows, and writes `pending_approval`. `place_order` refuses without a recorded human approver. Valio mock: `GET /products?q=` and `POST /orders` (assumption, verify Friday).

## 5. Risk engine
- **Discovery confidence:** logistic score over six features: source-type weight (partner 0.9, company site 0.7, news 0.6, directory 0.4); independent domain count; both names in quote; entity match tier (domain 1.0, name plus country 0.7, name only 0.3); recency within 12 months; Websets verified flag. Calibrated as described above.
- **Concentration:** severity `min(5, direct_suppliers)`.
- **Regulatory:** exact match on a seeded sanctions snapshot (assumption); EUDR and CSDDD as a per-supplier checklist; expired certificate gives severity 3.
- **Geopolitical:** country tier table; tier 3 or higher gives severity 3 to 4.
- **Live events and financial health:** classified monitor evidence, capped at severity 3 when the edge is below confidence 0.7.
- **Price premium:** published prices only; more than 15% above cheapest opted-in alternative gives severity 2.

## 6. Screens and graph view
1. **Discovery run:** live counter ("20 candidates, 11 resolved, 4 with quotes, 2 corroborated"). Candidate cards show name, confidence with calibration note, quote and URL. Rejections appear greyed with a reason.
2. **Supply map:** arrows supplier to buyer. Solid `agreed`, dashed `claimed`, dotted `inferred` with confidence, double `audited`. Node colour is max risk.
3. **Alert and alternatives:** alert with evidence; opted-in alternatives with price range and industry aggregate.
4. **Proposal and log:** Approve button, order status with Valio reference, audit log with evidence IDs.

## 7. Demo script (4 minutes)
- **0:00-0:25** Hotel login, supply map.
- **0:25-1:30** Click Discover upstream for Valio's dairy supplier. Live: counter ticks, two candidates appear with quotes and URLs. Judge clicks a URL.
- **1:30-2:00** Show calibration note and one rejected candidate ("only one domain").
- **2:00-2:30** Recall news on the dairy producer produces a risk alert with its quote.
- **2:30-3:00** Alternatives, price ranges, aggregate line.
- **3:00-3:30** Approve the proposal; Valio mock order placed.
- **3:30-3:50** Supplier confirms (claimed to agreed); auditor stamps audited.
- **3:50-4:00** Audit log showing evidence IDs per edge. Fallback: a pre-run discovery labelled with its run ID and timestamp.

## 9. Judging-criteria mapping
- **Context.dev (15%):** enrichment, extraction, news and monitor webhooks in the core flow; quotes and URLs on screen; monitors change confidence over time.
- **Valio (30%):** approval enforced in code; buyer need via upstream alert; controls via evidence IDs on every edge; exceptions via the rejection path; standards via JSON schemas.
- **Trust and privacy (10%):** candidates below 0.5 hidden from buyers; seller lists never exposed.
- **Concreteness (10%):** schemas, budgets and queries above.
- **Side bounties (10%):** Gemini, Exa as data source, Aiven, CRACI. No Codex.

## 10. Risks and cuts
- **Cut order:** live Websets, Exa Monitors, Oxylabs, Modal, Cala, ArcadeDB, price premium.
- **Latency over 60s:** run budgets and cache; demo uses a pre-warmed query.
- **Small calibration sample:** if the fit is poor, relabel the score as a heuristic and say so.
- **Webhook reachability:** poll fallback every 5 minutes.

## 11. Biggest weakness of this proposal (be honest)
Calibration rests on about 60 labels from one person, so the confidence numbers are only as honest as that sample. The live path depends on three external APIs; a failure removes the headline moment, so the cache is effectively the demo.
