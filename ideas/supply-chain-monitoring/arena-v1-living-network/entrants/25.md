# Entrant 25: Ravel Diligence, supply-chain due diligence for investors (lens: Investor / Cala angle)

## 0. Bold bet
Valio stays the primary flow (hotel alert, alternatives, approved order), and a second persona, an investor, runs supply-chain due diligence on a startup in the same network, with Cala supplying the company, funding and investor entities that the risk engine starts from. Cala is the spine of the DD report, not a sidebar, and the same network data powers both personas, which makes the demo feel like one product.

## 1. Summary and name
- **Ravel**: a living supply-chain network. Companies declare edges, agents add sourced `inferred` edges, and buyers and investors see only permitted risk, aggregates and published prices.
- Persona A: hotel procurement manager (Valio flow, as in the brief).
- Persona B: investor at a dummy fund, "Northwind Ventures", doing supply-chain DD on **OatNord** (dummy dairy-alternative startup, a Valio customer).
- Investor question: "Is OatNord's supply chain a risk to its next round?" Answer combines Cala (funding, investors, founders) with Ravel (suppliers, upstream risk, concentration).
- Assumption: Cala exposes company lookup (name or domain) and relations for funding rounds, investors and people, via API or MCP. Verify Friday morning. Fallback: a Cala snapshot JSON for the 10 demo companies, behind the same interface.

## 2. Architecture and DB choice
- **Postgres on Aiven** as system of record (Aiven bounty). Tables for the network, grants, risk, approvals and audit. No ArcadeDB in the core; it is a Cut-first item.
- **Cala bridge** (FastAPI service): resolves a company to a Cala id, fetches funding, investors and people, and caches results in `cala_entity` with `fetched_at` and source URL, so DD reports show provenance and stay fast.
- **Valio adapter** (FastAPI): product, availability and quote lookup, `request_order` (creates approval only) and `place_order` (requires approved approval id).
- **Agents** (Python, Gemini function calling): discovery, monitoring, procurement, each calling Exa, Context.dev and the internal tools.
- **Web app** (React + Cytoscape.js): hotel view, investor DD view, approval inbox, audit trail.
- Context.dev monitors send webhooks to the monitoring endpoint. Demo run is local or Aiven Runtime.
- Auth: persona API keys with scopes (`buyer:hotel`, `investor:northwind`, `approver`, `auditor-sim`). Every endpoint checks scope and logs to `audit_log`.

## 3. Data model and key queries
- `company(id, name, domain, country, industry, cala_id NULL, registered, opted_in_visible)`
- `cala_entity(cala_id, kind ENUM('company','round','investor','person'), payload JSONB, source_url, fetched_at)`
- `edge(id, from_id, to_id, trust ENUM('inferred','claimed','agreed','audited'), confidence, source_urls JSONB, product_id NULL)`
- `dd_grant(id, target_company_id, viewer_persona, scope ENUM('risk_summary','supplier_list'), expires_at, granted_by)`: default none. OatNord grants `supplier_list` to Northwind for 7 days; this is an explicit consent event.
- `risk_signal(id, company_id, type, severity, score, evidence_url, detected_at, source)`
- `price_publication(product_id, seller_id, price_min, price_max, published)`
- `approval(id, action JSONB, status, approver, decided_at)`, `audit_log(id, actor, tool, args_hash, result_summary, at)`

Visibility enforcement: the DD endpoint returns a projection. Investors see OatNord's Cala profile (public), its risk signals, supplier list only when a non-expired `dd_grant` exists, industry aggregates with k≥5, and never the hotel's private map.

Key queries:
- **Cala-to-network join:** `SELECT c.*, cc.payload FROM company c JOIN cala_entity cc ON cc.cala_id=c.cala_id WHERE c.id=$target;`
- **Upstream:** `WITH RECURSIVE up AS (SELECT from_id,to_id,1 d FROM edge WHERE to_id=$target UNION ALL SELECT e.from_id,e.to_id,up.d+1 FROM edge e JOIN up ON e.to_id=up.from_id WHERE up.d<4) SELECT * FROM up;`
- **Hidden concentration:** shared upstream across direct suppliers, `GROUP BY from_id HAVING count(DISTINCT to_id)>=2`.
- **Blast radius:** same recursion with `to_id=$failing`, count downstream buyers and portfolio companies (Cala investor links).
- **Runway flag:** last Cala round date older than 18 months with no successor round.
- **Alternatives:** opted-in suppliers with published price ranges, ordered by pgvector similarity on product embeddings.

## 4. Agents (discovery, monitoring, procurement)
- **Discovery.** Inputs: Exa search (company and news filters, domain allowlist) and Context.dev company enrichment for OatNord's named suppliers. Tool: `propose_edge`. Output: `inferred` edge, confidence, source URLs. Validation: at least 2 distinct URLs or confidence ≥0.6. Unregistered companies become nodes and an invite event. Cala entity lookup runs for every new company with a name match.
- **Monitoring.** Context.dev monitors on supplier domains and news queries, webhook to `/hooks/monitor`. Gemini classifies changes into risk types and writes `risk_signal` with evidence URL. Also refreshes Cala funding payloads daily and emits a runway flag if stale.
- **Procurement.** Input: upstream risk for the hotel and alternatives. Calls Valio adapter `search_products`, `check_availability`, `get_quote`. Output: order draft and reason. `request_order` creates an approval only. The agent cannot call `place_order`.
- Human approval: manager persona approves in the inbox. `place_order` checks the approval id and status, refuses otherwise, and logs both.

## 5. Risk engine
Score 0-100 per company and per investor view, with bands green <40, amber 40-69, red ≥70. Every component has evidence.
- **Concentration (30%):** single-source critical input, plus shared upstream count from section 3. Source: network edges.
- **Geopolitical (15%):** supplier country in a static risk table (assumption: public index seeded).
- **Regulatory (15%):** sanctions name match (seeded offline, assumption), certificate expiry in Valio synthetic data, EUDR flag for cocoa and coffee inputs.
- **Financial (20%):** runway flag and round history from Cala, layoffs and insolvency keywords from Context.dev news.
- **Live events (15%):** Context.dev monitors (recalls, strikes, outages).
- **Price premium (5%):** Valio price vs lowest published alternative, only when both are public.

## 6. Screens and graph view
- **Hotel view.** Supply map with Valio at centre; risk alert on the upstream dairy producer with clickable evidence URL; alternatives panel (opted-in, price ranges, industry aggregate); procurement proposal with Approve and Reject.
- **Investor DD view.** Header: OatNord from Cala (founders, last round, investors, source URL). Middle: Cytoscape map showing only what the grant allows, with direction arrows, edge style by trust (dashed `inferred`, dotted `claimed`, solid `agreed`, double `audited`), colour by risk, confidence badges. Right: DD score, signals, runway flag. Bottom: "Request supplier list" button, which creates a grant request.
- **Consent inbox.** OatNord approves the grant with an expiry.
- **Audit trail.** Every tool call, Cala lookup and grant event, with args hash.

## 7. Demo script (4 minutes)
1. **0:00-0:50.** Hotel login. Supply map. Live Context.dev run fires on the dairy producer's news and the alert appears with its live URL.
2. **0:50-1:40.** Alternatives, procurement proposal for a partial-availability SKU, substitution suggested by the Valio adapter, manager approves, order placed, audit line shown.
3. **1:40-2:30.** Switch to Northwind. OatNord DD view: Cala profile with funding and investors (source URL shown). Map is locked to the risk summary until consent.
4. **2:30-3:20.** OatNord approves a 7-day grant on its phone-width consent page. Supplier list unlocks. The dairy producer's risk flows into OatNord's DD score; runway flag is raised.
5. **3:20-3:50.** Supplier confirms an edge (`claimed` to `agreed`), auditor stamps `audited`.
6. **3:50-4:00.** Audit trail: Cala calls, grants, approvals and orders in one log.

## 9. Judging-criteria mapping
- **Valio (30%):** end-to-end order with approval gate and audit; exceptions via substitution; guardrails via scopes and `place_order` check.
- **Cala (bounty; criteria: need, execution, originality, core):** investor DD need is real; Cala payloads drive the DD header, runway flag and investor concentration; provenance URLs shown.
- **Context.dev (15%):** monitors and webhooks in the core live flow; live URL on screen.
- **Trust and privacy (10%):** consent grants with expiry, default private map, k≥5 aggregates.
- **Gemini (bounty):** function calling in all three agents.
- **Aiven (bounty):** managed Postgres and Runtime hosting.
- **CRACI (bounty):** CI with SBOM on the backend. Low effort.
- **Codex (bounty):** scaffolds for adapters and tests; log usage honestly.

## 10. Risks and cuts
- **Cut first:** ArcadeDB (never in), then portfolio-level features, then the Oxylabs option.
- **Cala risk:** API shape or access may differ from assumption. Mitigation: snapshot fallback behind same interface; the demo still shows Cala payloads with source URLs.
- **Focus risk:** two personas in 48h. Mitigation: investor view reuses hotel data and components; no separate backend.
- **Live monitor latency:** pre-triggered run plus backup video.

## 11. Biggest weakness of this proposal
It splits attention between two personas and depends on Cala's real API matching our assumptions. If Cala turns out to be a snapshot, the "Cala as core" claim weakens, and a judge may see the investor view as a second product bolted onto the Valio demo.
