# Entrant 50: Ravel Evidence, every edge and risk earns its confidence (lens: confidence calibration and source provenance)

## 0. Bold bet
Confidence is never a number the LLM invents. It is computed from verbatim, checked evidence snippets across independent sources, with decay over time, and every score on screen can be expanded into the exact quotes behind it. That makes the discovery and risk agents trustworthy enough to trigger a human-approved Valio order, which is the part judges will probe.

## 1. Summary and name
- **Ravel Evidence**: a living supply-chain network where agents propose `inferred` edges and risk signals, each backed by evidence rows (URL, retrieved time, verbatim snippet, content hash). Scores are derived, decay, and can be corroborated or contradicted.
- Trust levels (`inferred`, `claimed`, `agreed`, `audited`) remain a separate axis from confidence. Confidence applies to `inferred` edges and risk signals only.
- Demo network: Valio Aimo at centre, dummy hotel buyer, dummy dairy producer with real public pages (assumption: at least one real dairy company's public site and news are fetched live).

## 2. Architecture and DB choice
- **Postgres (Aiven)** as the single store. Evidence fits JSONB and relational joins; confidence is a SQL view. ArcadeDB is not needed for calibration and adds a second store to sync.
- Components: discovery and monitoring workers (Python), a **verifier** module (pure functions: snippet check, entity match, scoring), Valio adapter, web app.
- Exa (Search, Contents) and Context.dev (extract, monitors, webhooks) produce raw evidence. Gemini only proposes candidates and classifies; it never sets a score.
- Hosting: workers and API on Aiven Runtime; Context.dev webhook endpoint on the same app.

## 3. Data model and key queries
- `source(id, url, domain, registrable_domain, source_class ENUM('news','company_site','registry','partner_dataset','sanctions_list','catalogue','aggregator'), prior NUMERIC)`
- `evidence(id, source_id, url, retrieved_at, snippet TEXT, snippet_offset INT, content_hash, extractor_version, stance ENUM('supports','contradicts'), verified BOOL, observed_at)`
- `edge(id, from_id, to_id, trust ENUM('inferred','claimed','agreed','audited'), base_confidence, status, created_at)`
- `edge_evidence(edge_id, evidence_id)` and `signal_evidence(signal_id, evidence_id)`
- `risk_signal(id, company_id, type, severity, detected_at, status ENUM('open','resolved'), list_version, list_entry_id NULL)`
- Confidence is a view, `v_edge_confidence`, computed at query time, so decay is always current.

Key queries:
- **Confidence (noisy-OR over independent supporting sources, minus contradictions):**
  `SELECT e.id, 1 - exp(sum(ln(1 - s.prior * exp(-ln(2) * age_days / s.half_life) * ev.snippet_ok))) ...` implemented in SQL as `1 - PRODUCT(1 - term)` with `term = prior * 0.5^(age/half_life)`, using only evidence where `verified` is true and `DISTINCT ON (registrable_domain, content_hash)`.
- **Corroboration:** count distinct registrable domains with a verified supporting snippet, `HAVING count >= 2`.
- **Upstream traversal:** recursive CTE over edges where `trust IN ('agreed','audited')` or `confidence >= 0.5`, depth ≤4.
- **Hidden concentration:** shared upstream, `GROUP BY from_id HAVING count(DISTINCT to_id) >= 2`, each edge carrying its confidence band.
- **Alternatives:** opted-in suppliers with published prices; filtered by product match confidence.

Visibility: evidence snippets for edges touching a private map are shown only to the owning company. Buyers see only risk signals, their evidence, and aggregates (k≥5).

## 4. Agents (discovery, monitoring, procurement)
- **Discovery.** Inputs: Exa search with date and company filters, Exa Contents for full text, Context.dev company extract for named suppliers. Gemini returns a structured candidate: `{from, to, relation, quote, quote_url}` with a JSON schema. Verifier then checks: quote appears verbatim in fetched text (normalised whitespace), both entity names appear within 300 characters of the quote, source not on the blocklist. Failed candidates are logged, not dropped silently. Passing candidates become `inferred` edges with evidence rows.
- **Monitoring.** Context.dev monitors on supplier domains and news queries. Webhook payload stored as evidence with content hash. A change is a risk candidate only if the new text yields a verified snippet. Also re-observation of a known edge adds evidence and refreshes `observed_at`. If a page no longer contains a previously quoted snippet, that evidence is marked `stale`, not deleted.
- **Procurement.** Input: risk signals with status open and confidence ≥0.6, or a deterministic sanctions match. Calls Valio adapter `search_products`, `check_availability`, `get_quote`, then `request_order` which creates an approval only. The approval card lists the evidence used. `place_order` refuses unless approved.
- Human approval is the only path to `place_order`. Approval records the evidence ids, so the audit trail explains why.

## 5. Risk engine
- **Score pipeline:** type prior × per-evidence weight; confidence from the same noisy-OR formula; severity = type weight × confidence; band Possible (0.4-0.6), Likely (0.6-0.8), Strong (≥0.8).
- **Deterministic sources:** sanctions list match (exact name plus country) gives confidence 1.0 with `list_version` and entry id. Certificate expiry from Valio synthetic data gives 1.0 with the data record as source.
- **Geopolitical:** static country table with its own source row (assumption: public index).
- **Financial and live events:** news and monitor evidence through the same verifier. Layoffs, insolvency and recall keywords only produce candidates; the LLM label alone is never enough.
- **Concentration:** graph-derived; evidence is the edges themselves.
- **Price premium:** only seller-published prices; evidence is the price publication row.

Calibration (Friday night, 45 minutes): hand-label 40 candidate edges and risk signals from our own runs into correct or incorrect. Bin by confidence into three buckets and report the observed rate on the screen. Honest claim: small sample, but the bins are measured, not guessed.

## 6. Screens and graph view
- **Hotel supply map:** Cytoscape, arrows for direction, dashed `inferred`, solid `agreed`, double-line `audited`, `claimed` dotted. Edge label shows the band ("Likely 0.72") and a source count.
- **Evidence drawer:** click any edge or alert to see each source: domain, retrieved time, highlighted verbatim snippet, stance, decay indicator (fresh, aging, stale), and a "why this score" breakdown with the formula terms.
- **Risk alert:** signal, band, two top evidence cards, and a button "Re-check now", which triggers a Context.dev extract live.
- **Procurement approval:** proposed switch, evidence list, price range, Approve or Reject.
- **Audit trail:** every candidate, rejection reason (for example "quote not found"), approval and order.

## 7. Demo script (4 minutes)
1. **0:00-0:40.** Hotel supply map. Click an inferred edge: band, sources, highlighted snippet.
2. **0:40-1:30.** Risk alert on the dairy producer. Open evidence drawer; show a rejected candidate and why (verifier log).
3. **1:30-2:10.** Live run: press "Re-check now". Context.dev extract runs, a new snippet appears with its live URL, confidence updates on screen in real time.
4. **2:10-2:50.** Alternatives and procurement proposal, evidence listed on the card. Manager approves; order placed through Valio mock.
5. **2:50-3:30.** Supplier confirms edge (`claimed` to `agreed`, confidence shown as fixed 1.0 for agreed). Auditor stamps `audited`.
6. **3:30-4:00.** Calibration panel: measured bins from Friday labels, and one decayed edge turning "aging" after a simulated date shift.

## 9. Judging-criteria mapping
- **Valio (30%):** approval card explains "why" with evidence; audit trail is the traceable log; guardrails enforced in `place_order`.
- **Context.dev (15%):** monitors and extract in the live path; every new fact carries its URL and retrieved time.
- **Trust and privacy (10%):** confidence separated from trust; private-map evidence stays with its owner; aggregates k≥5.
- **Concreteness (10%):** SQL view, verifier rules and drawer contents are specified.
- **Gemini (bounty):** structured output for candidates, verified by code.
- **Aiven (bounty):** Postgres and Runtime hosting.
- **CRACI (bounty):** CI with SBOM. Low effort.
- **Feasibility (25%):** verifier is pure code, no extra service.

## 10. Risks and cuts
- **Cut first:** calibration on more than 40 items; decay UI animations; Oxylabs.
- **Risk:** verbatim-quote check fails often on rewritten or PDF text, so few candidates pass. Mitigation: pre-seeded dummy pages plus one real page known to pass; show rejected candidates honestly.
- **Risk:** Exa or Context.dev latency during the demo. Mitigation: pre-cached run plus live re-check with timeout and fallback to cached evidence labelled as cached.
- **Risk:** half-life constants are guesses. Label them as assumptions and say so.

## 11. Biggest weakness of this proposal
The provenance machinery is strongest where judges look least. Most of the demo's visible value still comes from the map and the approval button, and a team could spend the weekend on verifier edge cases. The priors and half-lives are assumptions, so the calibration story is only as honest as a 40-item sample.
