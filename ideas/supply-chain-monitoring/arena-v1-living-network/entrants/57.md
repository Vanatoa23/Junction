# Entrant 57: Pohjola Chain, a Finnish food chain the jury recognises (lens: Finnish and Nordic context)

## 0. Bold bet
Instead of a generic global supply graph, we build the network on the Finnish food chain people in Helsinki already know: Valio's dairy farms, regional cooperatives, Ruokavirasto recall notices and Finnish-language news, all read live and in Finnish and English. Judges see their own world in the graph, and every live source is a page they can open in Finnish.

## 1. Summary and name
- Name: **Pohjola Chain** (working name; "Pohjola" is a common Finnish word, so check trademark before any public use).
- Product: a supply graph for a Finnish food-service buyer. Its dairy upstream runs through milk farms and regional cooperatives, and every edge and risk carries a Finnish or EU source URL. Buyers see risk warnings and opted-in alternatives only.
- Assumptions (to verify day 1):
  - PRH open company data (YTJ/avoindata) is reachable for basic company lookup by Business ID.
  - Ruokavirasto recall and withdrawal pages are readable through Context.dev scrape or monitor.
  - Yle and Kauppalehti news is reachable through Context.dev news search or Exa.
  - Finnish national sanctions data sits alongside the EU consolidated list (assumption: use the EU list as the authority; use OpenSanctions as a cross-check).
- All Finnish company data in the demo is either dummy or public and labelled with its URL.

## 2. Architecture and DB choice
- **Postgres on Aiven** as primary store. Recursive CTEs cover upstream traversal. ArcadeDB is not needed for a graph this size (about 60 demo nodes, a few hundred live-discovered ones) and it adds a second engine to set up.
- Components: Next.js UI in Finnish and English (i18n from day 1); FastAPI backend; agent worker in the same repo; Context.dev SDK for scrape, enrichment, news and monitors; Gemini for agent reasoning (Finnish prompts and extraction); Valio mock client; Aiven Valkey for the agent job queue and cache.
- Data flow: monitor webhook or agent run writes a `source_item` (URL, fetched_at, hash, language) → extraction to candidate facts → validation → `risk_signal` or `supply_edge` → SSE push to the browser.
- Each run saves the raw page snapshot and hash, so every claim can be replayed in the audit log.

## 3. Data model and key queries
- `company(id, name, name_fi_norm, business_id, country, municipality, kind: farm|coop|processor|wholesaler|hotel, registered, optin_visible)`
- `supply_edge(id, from_id, to_id, product_fi, trust, confidence, sources jsonb, created_at)`. Trust is `inferred|claimed|agreed|audited`. An `inferred` edge requires confidence and at least one URL (CHECK constraint).
- `source_item(id, url, publisher, lang, fetched_at, hash)`
- `risk_signal(id, company_id, type, severity, score, title_fi, title_en, source_item_id)`
- Visibility: view `v_buyer_edges` returns the buyer's incoming edges and any upstream node with an open signal. Private farm-to-coop edges stay hidden.
- Finnish Business ID check: the 7-digit body is weighted 7,9,10,5,8,4,2, summed, reduced mod 11, with the check digit from the remainder (assumption: standard rule, verify on day 1). Invalid IDs are rejected before any lookup.

```sql
-- Upstream closure with tier, from the hotel (Valio is tier 1)
WITH RECURSIVE up(id, tier, path) AS (
  SELECT e.from_id, 1, ARRAY[e.to_id, e.from_id] FROM supply_edge e WHERE e.to_id = :hotel
  UNION ALL
  SELECT e.from_id, up.tier+1, up.path || e.from_id
  FROM up JOIN supply_edge e ON e.to_id = up.id
  WHERE up.tier < 5 AND NOT (e.from_id = ANY(up.path))
) SELECT id, MIN(tier) FROM up GROUP BY id;

-- Dairy regional concentration: farms and coops that reach the same processor
SELECT processor_id, COUNT(DISTINCT coop_id) FROM closure_by_coop
GROUP BY processor_id HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT coop_id) >= 2;
```
- Blast radius: reverse closure from a failed coop, returning hotels and grocers with depth.
- Alternatives: opted-in suppliers with product category match and a published price range.

## 4. Agents (discovery, monitoring, procurement)
- **Discovery (Finnish sources first).** Inputs: a Finnish company name or Business ID typed by the judge; a seed list of dairy coops and farms. Tools: PRH open data lookup; Context.dev enrichment and news search (Finnish and English queries); Ruokavirasto page scrape. Output: `inferred` edge with confidence (name match plus region plus product) and source URLs. Validation: Business ID valid, two URLs or one register record, language recorded.
- **Monitoring.** Triggers: Context.dev monitors with webhooks on the Ruokavirasto recall page and selected Yle and Kauppalehti items; a daily delta re-check of supplier records in PRH. Output: `risk_signal` with Finnish and English titles. Each signal links the snapshot.
- **Procurement.** Trigger: recall or high signal on an upstream dairy node. Steps: find opted-in dairy alternatives in the same municipality or region; check the alternative passes the same sanctions and recall screen; show published prices; manager approves; Valio mock API creates the order. The audit log records each step in Finnish and English.

## 5. Risk engine
- **Recall and food safety:** Ruokavirasto notices. Severity critical when the product matches a supplied product code or name; high on regional notices. Source: the notice URL.
- **Sanctions:** EU consolidated list and OpenSanctions cross-check on each chain node, name plus Business ID. Matches go to a review queue; a match changes risk, not edge trust.
- **Regulatory and compliance:** certification expiry from Valio catalogue attributes; CSDDD flag from supplier declaration. EUDR is not applied to dairy.
- **Geopolitical:** static country table with sources; news signals from Yle and Kauppalehti.
- **Concentration:** shared-coop query and single-source flag, shown as a regional concentration warning.
- **Financial health:** PRH registry changes (status, company form changes, dissolution notices) plus Kauppalehti news on layoffs or insolvency. Assumption: the registry exposes status changes.
- **Live events:** strikes, outages and negative news from Yle items via monitors.
- **Price premium:** published prices only.
- Scoring: max of critical signals, else weighted sum mapped to low, medium, high, critical.

## 6. Screens and graph view
- **Language:** Finnish is the default; English toggle. Every alert shows both titles.
- **Graph (Cytoscape.js):** direction left to right, upstream farms and coops on the left, Valio centre, the hotel on the right. Tier columns by depth. Edge trust: dashed inferred, grey claimed, solid blue agreed, thick navy audited. Risk ring on nodes. Municipality shown on hover.
- **Home:** the hotel's supply map, with a "Tänään" (today) panel listing live items with their URLs.
- **Alert detail:** Finnish recall notice, linked product, affected batch, alternatives, source URL.
- **Alternatives and approval:** cards with price range and region; approval screen with Finnish and English text.
- **Audit timeline:** each event with source URL and snapshot time.
- **Supplier and auditor views:** confirm and stamp buttons.

## 9. Judging-criteria mapping
- **Valio (30%):** approval-gated order through the mock API; alternatives with published prices; audit timeline. Exception handling via recall screen.
- **Feasibility (25%):** Postgres only; Finnish sources are scraped or public, so no bespoke integrations; seeded dummy coops make the story reliable.
- **Context.dev (15%):** Ruokavirasto and news monitors with webhooks in the core flow, plus live enrichment.
- **Trust and privacy (10%):** trust and confidence shown per edge; private farm-to-coop edges hidden; anonymised aggregates only.
- **Concreteness (10%):** SQL, Business ID rule, alert titles.
- **Side bounties (10%):** Gemini for Finnish extraction, Aiven, Codex, CRACI. Cala skipped.

## 10. Risks and cuts
- Cut order: Oxylabs, Exa, the price premium signal, PRH delta job, English UI (keep Finnish with English alert titles).
- Risks: Finnish sources blocking scrapers or changing layout (mitigate: pre-fetched snapshots labelled as recorded, plus one live URL); PRH API access or rate limits (mitigate: cache the demo companies); Finnish trademark on the name; the recall notice in the demo must be a labelled demo notice, never a real product recall presented as real.

## 11. Biggest weakness of this proposal
The Finnish angle only pays off if the local sources work on stage. If Ruokavirasto or PRH is slow or blocked, the live beat falls back to recorded snapshots, and judges can tell. Our Finnish language support is also limited to what we can test in 48 hours, and the sanctions authority (EU list versus Finnish national lists) needs checking on day 1.
