# Junction 2026 supporters — practical research notes

**Checked: 24 October 2026.** These nine organizations appear under **Other supporters** in the [official roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners). They are not listed there as challenge owners or tech-platform partners. Company/site descriptions below are first-party evidence; suggestions for approaching them are our assessment. No contact was made.

The separate “Participants from these organizations” strip is not a sponsor list. Stanford, MIT, KTH, ETH Zürich and other names appearing only there are therefore excluded from partner coverage. Aaltoes and some other organizations appear in both sections and are counted only once.

## Lifeline Ventures

**Practical use: Early-stage investor conversation.** Its site presents founder support and a portfolio including technology companies. Use a short discussion to test who buys the proposed product, why the team has an advantage and what evidence a pilot needs. Do not spend build hours producing a funding deck. No Junction investment offer, judge role or office-hours allocation was verified.

Source: [official site](https://www.lifelineventures.com) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## FR8

**Practical use: Builder community.** FR8 describes a community for ambitious builders, scientists and problem-solvers. Useful for feedback, finding someone who has solved an unfamiliar technical problem and post-event connections. Bring a specific question and a working demo. No compute, workspace entitlement or technical API was established.

Source: [official site](https://fr8.so) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## Wave Ventures

**Practical use: Investor feedback.** Its official site identifies it as a next-generation venture-capital organization with Helsinki and Stockholm contact locations. The reviewed page is sparse; do not infer cheque sizes, programme eligibility or named attendees. Ask about the weakest commercial assumption in the project and the next validation step. No automatic funding pathway was verified.

Source: [official site](https://wave.ventures) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## AROC (Aalto Robotics Club)

**Practical use: Hardware knowledge.** The club describes hands-on robotics and projects including drones and autonomous racers. It is separate from Aalto University's challenge ownership. A focused compatibility/debugging question could be valuable if club members are available. Do not assume equipment loans, a particular mentor or access to club facilities from the supporter logo.

Source: [official site](https://site.aaltoroboticsclub.fi) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## Thinkin' Rocks

**Practical use: Hardware prototyping community.** The organization describes an applied engineering lab spanning software, hardware and research, with tools, space and a builder community. This makes it relevant to feasibility and prototyping questions. Ask what can actually be accessed during Junction. No particular fabrication service or guaranteed equipment booking was verified.

Source: [official site](https://thinkinrocks.com) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## Founders House Helsinki

**Practical use: Availability must be clarified.** The official Junction roster supplies the name and link. The linked site exposed only its title in both web extraction and a direct fetch. Current programmes, membership, services and event representatives could not be verified. Treat it as a possible networking contact; ask organizers what support is available. Do not invent an accelerator, accommodation or funding offer.

Source: [official site](https://founders-house.fi) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## Major League Hacking

**Practical use: Hackathon ecosystem and conduct.** The official site describes hackathons and developer programmes. Junction's page separately links the MLH Code of Conduct. Follow the actual Junction submission and event instructions; do not import prizes, sponsor packs or rules from another MLH event. Useful questions concern event support and where authoritative participant guidance is published.

Source: [official site](https://mlh.io) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## Aaltoes

**Practical use: Entrepreneurship community.** Aaltoes describes a student-run entrepreneurship society and programmes/community around Aalto and the Finnish startup ecosystem. It is distinct from Aalto University and does not identify the university challenge owner. Useful for feedback and follow-on community connections. No programme admission, grants or event-specific service was verified.

Source: [official site](https://aaltoes.com) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## XR Creator Con

**Practical use: XR creator community.** XRCC describes AI/XR hackathons and a spatial-computing community. It may be a useful source of interface and demo feedback if our project uses Rokoko or XR. Its own events and benefits are separate from Junction. Do not assume headset loans, SDK access or a Junction XR prize.

Source: [official site](https://www.xrcc.events/) · Role: [Junction roster](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners).

## Suggested approach

Protect building time. Choose at most a few relevant conversations: a technical obstacle for hardware communities, user/adoption feedback for founder communities, or commercial assumptions for investors. Prepare a 30-second explanation and one specific question. Record useful answers, their source and whether they change the implementation.

No supporter is assumed to provide free services, become a customer, judge the project or invest. The scope of the research is the currently published roster, not a promise that no additional supporters will join.
