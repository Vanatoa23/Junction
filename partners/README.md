## Evidence and limitations

- The authoritative roster is the [official event partner section](https://2026.hackjunction.com/#partners), read on 24 October. Its HTML contains named logo alt text and destination links even though ordinary text extraction omits them.
- Main: 2; Challenge: 16; Tech: 13; separately featured Google DeepMind: 1. Other supporters: 9.
- Google DeepMind is featured separately; no main/challenge/tech tier is inferred.
- The event still marks challenge tracks **TBA**. Project ideas are hypotheses; no report should be read as an announced challenge.
- New reports are source-backed operational briefs. Scailab and Founders House have explicitly documented evidence limitations.
- No authenticated integration, credit redemption, hardware use or performance benchmark was executed during this research.
- Coverage is complete for this dated roster. The organizer says more partners are being confirmed; recheck when briefs are released.

## How to use the reports

Select the user problem after reading the real brief. Confirm data/tool access, define a measurable baseline and keep the first prototype narrow. Do not combine partners merely for logo count. Preserve the distinction between public product capabilities, actual participant access and proposed implementation.
