# Round 3, match 02: verdict

**Winner: e09** (the five-beat story on Today, with the map beside it)

e09 wins because a non-technical reader gets the whole situation from one headline sentence and a five-beat timeline without learning any screen layout, and that matches criterion 1 better than e15's three numbers and five screens. Its map explains itself at every zoom: the hub line is labelled "Why: Port of Rotterdam closed.", the zoomed-out view clusters into numbered bubbles, and the offline fallback is a plain world outline with the same dots and lines. e15 has a polished look, but its Alert page is a side-by-side layout with an Undo window and a separate Map tab, which adds more to build in one day and gives less of a clear "why" on the map. e09 is also calmer (paper background, one motion only) and has fewer moving parts to build.

**Ideas worth stealing from e15 (loser):**
1. **Alert fact row under the headline** ("3 suppliers affected · Noticed 2 minutes after publication · High"). It answers "how fast we noticed" and "how bad" in one line, which is exactly what the Head of Sales asks.
2. **Risk score as a large number inside a thin ring, with the level word beneath** ("72 / High risk"). It gives a glanceable score while keeping the words as the primary signal, and it stays within the one-day build.
