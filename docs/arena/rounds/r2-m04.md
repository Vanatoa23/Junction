# Round 2, match 04: verdict

**Winner: e15** (Calm bank statement)

**Why:** The first-priority test is whether a non-technical Head of Sales can answer "are we affected, how badly, since when" in seconds, and e15's Today screen does that with three big numbers (Suppliers, Need attention, Ready to approve) above the live alert. e15 also explains its add-supplier flow more concretely, with a name-only save and nine optional chips that reveal one field per tap, and its risk panel ("Based on 8 facts. Could not check ownership.") is honest about gaps. e13 is the more beautiful and calmer page, but its status-by-shape map (filled, hollow, ringed, dashed) asks a non-expert to decode symbols before they understand the situation, and its add form reveals every field at once.

**Best idea from the loser to steal (e13):** Red is reserved strictly for danger, and nothing else on the page uses it. Applying that rule to e15's palette would keep its blue accent and green/amber pills while making a red risk signal unmistakable.
