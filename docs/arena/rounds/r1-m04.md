# Round 1, match 04: e07 vs e08

## Winner: e08 (Risk score is the hero)

e08 wins because a non-technical reader gets the whole story from the map in a glance: each supplier is a circle sized and coloured by risk, with one plain line saying why. Its add flow is the simplest of the two, a single "Supplier name" field with Enter to add and optional fields behind one quiet link, which meets the brief's "simple with optional extras" without the parsing work e07 implies. It is also the more feasible build in one day of React and Leaflet, since e07 needs natural-language chip extraction and a follow-up question flow before the map is useful. e07 has real strengths in its zoom behaviour and honest wording, but its conversational box is the riskier and slower-to-understand part.

## Best idea of the loser worth stealing (e07)

The one-sentence hub caption on a disruption, for example "Two suppliers are affected by the Port of Rotterdam closure", placed beside the company hub. It tells a Head of Supply the cause and the impact in the same place the map shows them, and e08 could adopt it in place of its shorter hub label.

## Scores by criterion (brief order)

1. Understood in seconds: e08 (risk circles plus a reason line). e07 is clear but needs the colour meaning explained.
2. Calm, polished look: near tie. Both are light and restrained.
3. Map explains itself at both zooms, direct lines only: slight edge to e07 on zoom detail (curved lines when zoomed out, straight lines with names when zoomed in, hub caption). e08 matches it with country totals and per-circle reasons.
4. Simple add flow with optional extras: e08, clearly.
5. Alerts and alternatives, clear and honest: near tie. e07's "Approved by you" and "No information yet" wording is slightly more honest. e08 collapses "Ruled out" by default, which hides the reason.
6. Feasible in one day: e08.
