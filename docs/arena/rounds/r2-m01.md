# Round 2, match 01 verdict

**Winner: e01 (Map-first)**

Why: e01 gives a Head of Supply or Sales one screen with one idea, the map, and it explains itself at both zoom levels (markers merge when zoomed out, names and thicker lines appear when zoomed in) with lines drawn only to direct suppliers. Its risk display shows a word badge and plain sentences with the 0 to 100 number hidden in a tooltip, and its add-supplier sheet reveals one field at a time after the name, which fits the "optional, never forced" rule better than e03's panel with several fields visible. Its light, daylight palette and single pulse read as calmer and more buildable in one day than e03's dark, glowing, breathing-line control room, which also adds three screens, a left rail and a visible "Medium, 58" score that a non-expert does not need.

Best idea from the loser (e03) to steal: the one-line map caption, "Lines show direct suppliers. Colour shows risk.", which states the map's rule in plain words on the map itself. A close second is its "Why?" link that reveals the source and date behind each risk reason, which makes the reasons more honest without adding numbers to the main view.
