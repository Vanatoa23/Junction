# Round 5, final: e09 vs e25

**Winner: e25 ("The map is the beauty")**

## Why

e25 puts the company on a calm map as the hub, draws lines only to direct suppliers, and spells out what happens at country zoom (bubbles) and at city zoom (names and cities), which is the brief's third and most specific requirement. Its disruption cue shows where the problem is (a soft red area over Rotterdam) and why (the hub line "a port closure at Rotterdam affects 3 of your suppliers"), so a non-technical reader gets the picture in seconds without reading a timeline. e09 has the best single sentence for the first screen and a clear five-beat story for "since when," but its map is underspecified at local zoom and its story pushes the answer below the headline. e25 also keeps colour reserved for risk, uses a side panel so the map never disappears, and records an approval without changing anything else.

## Ideas from e09 to merge into e25

1. Five-beat timeline in the Alerts view (We noticed, We checked, Suppliers affected, Replacements found, You approved), with the current beat highlighted, to answer "since when" and "how fast."
2. The one-sentence headline on the Map screen ("Port of Rotterdam is closed. 3 of your suppliers are affected.") as the alert line above the map.
3. Explicit add-supplier result lines: "Found: country, website, 2 facts." and "Could not find: yearly spend."
4. An offline fallback: a plain grey world outline with the same dots and lines, as the brief requires.
5. A "How we know" link that lists sources in everyday language.
6. A timestamped approval confirmation ("Approved by you at 10:42. Recorded.") in place of a generic success message.
