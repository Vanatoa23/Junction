# Round 2, match 03 verdict

**Winner: e09** (the five-beat story on Today, with the map beside it)

**Why e09 wins.** It answers the brief's "how fast we noticed" and "how serious" in one vertical timeline with times, so a non-technical reader gets the whole incident in under a minute. Its map explains itself at both zooms: numbered clusters when zoomed out, labelled pins with straight lines when zoomed in, and a hub line that reads "Why: Port of Rotterdam closed." The add flow is the cleaner of the two, with one required field, optional chips that each reveal one field, and a two-line result ("Found... / Could not find..."), and the editorial paper palette feels calmer and more distinct than e12's conventional SaaS look. e12 is strong on decision-readiness, but its add flow contradicts itself ("Add saves immediately" followed by an "Add it now" button), and grouping markers by country at zoomed-out level needs custom logic that e09's clustering does not.

**Best idea of the loser worth stealing (e12).** The Today screen puts the single top safe replacement in one filled button ("Approve Gdansk Metals"), followed by an "Undo" and a short "Decisions" log. That makes the approve moment both faster and more honest, and it should be folded into e09's "You approved" beat.
