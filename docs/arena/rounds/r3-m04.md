# Round 3, match m04: verdict

**Winner: e25 ("The map is the beauty")**

e25 wins because it is the calmer and more explicit design on the criteria that come after first-glance clarity. Its palette is quiet, and colour appears only where risk does, so the stage screen reads as an atlas rather than a dashboard. Its map behaviour is spelled out at both zoom levels (merged bubbles coloured by worst risk when zoomed out, names and cities when zoomed in), and the disruption is explained on the hub line ("a port closure at Rotterdam affects 3 of your suppliers"). Both designs are understandable in seconds, so the tie on criterion 1 falls to criterion 2, where e25 is clearly stronger.

**Where e32 is the stronger one:** its "Today" tab answers the Head of Sales question ("are we affected?") before any map, and its add-supplier result card ("We found 5 things. We could not find 2", with an "Add city" prompt) is a more honest and more concrete version of the found/not-found message. These are close, and they do not outweigh e25's visual calm and map clarity.

**Two ideas from e32 worth stealing:**
1. The add-supplier result card that lists what was found and what could not be found, with a single "Add city" prompt for the gap. It turns the brief's "shows what it found and what it could not" into a concrete, honest moment.
2. The 10-second "Undo" after approving a replacement. It keeps the approve moment one click while making it reversible, which adds honesty to the decision step.
