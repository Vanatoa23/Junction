# Round 1 (m15): verdict

**Winner: e29 (Two-level zoom story)**

**Why:** e29 is the faster read for a Head of Supply or Sales: a calm map, three plain screens, and a one-line promise ("problems are never hidden in a crowd"). Its map answers criterion 3 most directly, with an explicit three-level zoom rule, a single clustering threshold (40 px), and the rule that affected suppliers never cluster, so an alert stays visible at every zoom. e30 is more honest about uncertainty, but it puts a source line and a confidence note under nearly every sentence, which costs seconds of reading for a non-technical user and makes the screens busier.

**Best idea of the loser worth stealing (e30):** label every claim with its basis in plain words, such as "Confirmed by 2 sources" or "One source so far; still checking", and show "We could not confirm its ports, so this score may be too low" as a quiet note. That gives e29's alerts and risk panel the honesty it currently lacks, without adding clutter.
