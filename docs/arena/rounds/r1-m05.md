# Round 1, judge m05: verdict

**Winner: e09** (Calm morning briefing, desktop first, five-beat story)

## Why e09 wins

e09 answers the Head of Sales' question, "are we affected, how badly, since when," in one headline and one timeline: "Port of Rotterdam is closed. 3 of your suppliers are affected," then five timed beats from "We noticed" to "You approved." It matches the brief's stage-first, desktop-first direction, with Today, Suppliers and Replacements in plain words and a single hidden-in-plain-sight presenter button. Its map rules (dots merge into numbered clusters when zoomed out, become labelled pins with straight lines when zoomed in, and only direct suppliers are joined to the hub) and its risk wording (level in words first, at most three reasons, "How we know") are specific enough to build in a day.

e10 is strong on touch and honest undo, but it leads with a tablet and a 72px bottom bar when the brief says desktop first, and it hides the presenter control behind a long press, which is a poor fit for a live stage demo. Its 20px minimum body text also makes the screens feel less calm and editorial than e09's paper-and-Inter system.

## Best idea of the loser worth stealing (e10)

Approval shows "Approved and recorded" with an Undo for five seconds, which makes the approve moment honest and reversible. Tapping a risk reason to highlight its place on the map is also worth adopting in e09, since it links the explanation to the map at every zoom level.
