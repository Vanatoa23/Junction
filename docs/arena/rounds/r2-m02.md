# Round 2, match 02: e05 vs e08

## Winner: e08 (Risk score is the hero)

e08 wins because a Head of Supply or Sales can read it in seconds: the home screen is the map, each supplier is a coloured circle with a one-line reason beside it, and the add-supplier flow is a single field where Enter adds the supplier at once. e05 is charming, but its five-question first-run setup and its zoomed-in map that shows only names push the explanation onto a card, so it asks more of the user before the picture makes sense. e08 also explains itself at local zoom (reason on each circle), keeps the replacement moment to one large button, and stays honest with "Checked 2 min ago" and "We could not find" with a retry.

## Best idea of the loser to steal (e05)

Hide the 0 to 100 number entirely outside a tooltip and lead every supplier with a level word plus full-sentence reasons, each with a grey source line ("public news, 3 days ago"). That keeps e08's map-first clarity while avoiding a bare number that a non-technical reader has to interpret.
