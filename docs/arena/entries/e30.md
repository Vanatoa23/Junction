# e30: Trust and honesty

## Concept
Every statement says where it came from and how sure we are, in plain words. The feeling is a careful colleague who says "we found this, here is where, and we could not confirm that."

## Navigation and screens
Three screens: "Suppliers" (home: map and list), "Alerts", and "Supplier" (opened from either, with reasons and replacements). A top bar shows the company name and, at the far right, a small "Publish test notice" control for the presenter, labelled as a test. Nothing else.

## Add-supplier flow
A panel slides in from the right. Name is the only required field. A collapsed "Add more if you know it" group holds the rest: Website, Country, City, What they supply, How critical, Yearly spend, Lead time, Contact, Notes. A pasted list makes one editable card per line. After saving, "What we found" lists each fact with its source, and "We could not find" lists gaps in grey.

## Map
The company is a navy hub. Thin grey lines run only to direct suppliers. Markers: ready teal, checking amber outline, needs your choice amber fill, could not find grey dashed. Zoomed out, markers group by country with counts; zoomed in, names appear. A click opens a side card with score and reasons. A disruption turns the supplier and its line red; the hub caption gives the reason and source: "A port it relies on is closed. Source: port authority, 10 minutes ago."

## Risk and explanation
Show "62 of 100, Medium". Each reason is one sentence with a small grey source line: "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam. Source: public shipping schedules, checked today." Where a check failed: "We could not confirm its ports, so this score may be too low." Uncertainty is a quiet note: never hidden, never alarming.

## Alerts and alternatives
Alert rows show what happened, where, affected suppliers, first seen, and either "Confirmed by 2 sources" or "One source so far; still checking." Alternatives are ranked cards with one label: "Safe to approve" (green), "Needs a check" (amber), or "Ruled out" (grey, with reason). Each card has one sentence on why and a source line. One button: "Approve this replacement", followed by "Recorded today at 14:02 by you."

## Visual style
Light theme. Background #F8FAFC, panels #FFFFFF, text #0F172A, secondary text #475569, lines #CBD5E1, hub #1E3A5F, accent teal #0F766E, caution amber #B45309, disruption red #B91C1C. Inter: 28px headings, 16px body, 13px source lines in #64748B. 8px spacing grid. One gentle fade when an alert arrives; no other motion.

## What we deliberately leave out
Confidence percentages, provider names, raw search results, deeper supplier tiers on the map, login, red alarm banners, and any claim without a source. A claim with no source does not appear.
