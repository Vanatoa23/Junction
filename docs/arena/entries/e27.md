# e27: Accessibility first

## 1. Concept
A calm control room that anyone can read from across a meeting table or with a screen reader. Every risk is stated in words and shape first; colour only reinforces it. Large type, short sentences, nothing that depends on spotting a small dot.

## 2. Navigation and screens
Three screens, named plainly: **Today** (default), **Suppliers**, **Alternatives** (opened from an alert). A top bar holds the company name, the three tabs, and a small **Presenter** button at the far right. The first focusable item is "Skip to alerts". Everything works without a mouse: Tab moves between regions, arrow keys move through lists, Enter opens, Esc closes.

## 3. Add-supplier flow
A single-column side sheet with 18px labels above fields. Order: Name (the only required field, autofocused), Website, Country, City, What they supply. A collapsed "Add more details (optional)" disclosure holds criticality, yearly spend, lead time, contact and notes. Pasting a list shows one name per line with a preview before saving. After saving, a plain sentence reports the result: "Found 3 facts. Could not find: contact details." Results are text, never icons alone.

## 4. Map
The hub is a dark filled circle with a white ring labelled "Your company". Each direct supplier is a labelled marker; names appear from zoom level 5 onward. Lines are solid when normal and dashed when disrupted. Nearby suppliers cluster into a count badge when zoomed out; activating the badge zooms in. Clicking a supplier opens a right-hand panel with its name, risk level in words and its reasons. A disruption draws a hatched outline around the affected area with the text label "Port of Rotterdam closed"; affected lines turn thicker and dashed. Pan and zoom work from the keyboard (+, -, arrows), and a text list of suppliers sits beside the map for screen readers.

## 5. Risk and explanation
Three levels, always shown as word plus symbol: **Low** (circle, blue), **Medium** (triangle, amber), **High** (square, vermillion, heavy outline). The score reads as text: "High risk: 78 of 100." Reasons are up to three sentences, each starting with a fact: "Two of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." No jargon, and no hover-only text.

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
Today lists alerts as plain cards: a title sentence, the affected supplier names, "Noticed 4 minutes ago", and the level word. Alternatives are a numbered list led by a status word, "Safe to approve", "Needs a check" or "Ruled out", each followed by one reason sentence. The approve button is 56px tall and reads "Approve Halden Steel as replacement". After approval, a confirmation sentence appears in a live region and is announced. Ruled-out options stay visible, disabled, with their reason.

## 7. Visual style
Light only, with contrast checked against text.
- Background #F7F7F4; surface #FFFFFF
- Text #1B1F24 (about 17:1); secondary text #4A525C (about 8:1)
- Hub #1B1F24
- Low #0072B2; Medium text #9A5B00; High #B93A00 (both above 5:1 on white)
- Focus ring #0B57D0, 3px, never removed

Typography: Inter with a system-ui fallback. Body 18px, no text below 16px, headings 28px, line height 1.5. Spacing on an 8px grid; click and tap targets at least 44px. Mood: paper-calm, generous white space. Motion is one 300ms fade, switched off entirely under reduced-motion.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
Hover-only tooltips, red-versus-green pairs, tier-two lines, tiny map labels, charts and number dashboards, technical terms, animation beyond the single fade, and any login.
