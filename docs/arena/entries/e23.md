# e23: Professional but dense

## 1. Concept
A trading-desk calm: one ordered screen where every supplier, risk and alert is in view at once, with plain sentences doing the explaining. Dense for the expert eye, never cryptic, because every number carries a word.

## 2. Navigation and screens
Top bar with three tabs: **Overview** (default), **Suppliers**, **Alerts (n)**. A grey "Test a disruption" button sits far right, quiet until the presenter needs it. Four screens in total: Overview, Suppliers, Alert detail, Add supplier (a panel, not a page).

## 3. Add-supplier flow
"Add supplier" opens a side panel. Fields in order: Company name (only required field), Website, Country, City (suggestions follow the country), What they supply, How critical (Low / Medium / High, default Medium). A collapsed "More details" group holds yearly spend, lead time in days, contact and notes. "Paste a list" offers one name per line; each becomes a row marked Checking. After saving, one honest line: "Found: country, city, 3 facts. Could not find: website."

## 4. Map
Muted light-grey tiles, offline fallback in #C9CFD6. The hub is the company, a navy square. Lines are 1px grey curves to direct suppliers only.
- **Zoomed out:** markers cluster with a count, coloured by the worst supplier inside. Lines stay, labels hide.
- **Zoomed in:** names appear, lines thicken, clusters split.
- **Click a marker:** a card slides in with name, status, risk level and one reason sentence. Two buttons: "Open", "See alternatives".
- **Disruption:** an amber translucent area appears over the incident with a plain label ("Port of Rotterdam: strike"). Affected suppliers and their lines turn red. The hub card reads "3 suppliers affected".

## 5. Risk and explanation
Score shown as a number, a bar and a word: Low (0-33), Medium (34-66), High (67-100). Colour is never the only signal. Beneath, up to three reasons written as sentences: "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." Each reason ends with "checked 14 Nov, 09:12". Unknowns get their own line: "We could not check: ownership."

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
The Alerts list rows read: headline, suppliers affected, severity word, "noticed 4 min after publication". Alert detail has three bands: a one-sentence summary, the map zoomed to the incident, then an alternatives table.
- Columns: Supplier, status badge ("Safe to approve" green, "Needs a check" amber, "Ruled out" grey), one-sentence reason, action.
- Ranked by safety; ruled-out rows sit last, greyed, with their reason still visible.
- **Approve moment:** one primary button, "Approve replacement". After one click the row reads "Approved by you, 14 Nov, 09:20" and is written to the decision log. No confirmation dialog.

## 7. Visual style
Light mode only.
- Background #F5F6F4; surface #FFFFFF; border #D9DDE3; ink #1B2430; secondary text #5B6675.
- Hub navy #1F3A5F; accent and buttons #2F6FB2.
- Safe #2E7D4F; needs a check #B26A00; high risk #B3261E; ruled out #8A929C.
- Typography: Inter. 13px tables with tabular numbers, 15px body, 20px headings.
- Spacing on a 4px grid; table rows 36px; cards padded 16px.
- Mood: quiet market terminal. One pulse animation on a new alert, nothing else moves.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
Dark mode, login, price charts or tickers, drawn deeper tiers, a settings page, export, multi-company switching, tooltips longer than one sentence, and any technical label.
