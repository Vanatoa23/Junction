# e12: Head of Supply lens

## 1. Concept
Open the app and read what to do now, with the best replacement already picked. When nothing is wrong, the screen says so quietly; when something is, one decision waits for one click.

## 2. Navigation and screens
A left rail with three items: **Today** (opens first), **Suppliers** (map and list together), **Add supplier**. Rail footer: "Show a news notice" for the presenter.

## 3. Add-supplier flow
1. **Name**: the only required field, focused on open.
2. **Add** saves immediately.
3. A quiet line offers five optional chips: Website, Country and city, What they supply, How critical, Yearly spend. Each chip opens one field.
4. A "Paste a list" tab takes one name per line.
5. Result: "Found 6 facts. Could not check yearly spend." Button: "Add it now."

## 4. Map
- **Hub**: dark teal circle labelled "Our company", at the centre.
- **Lines**: one thin grey line per direct supplier only.
- **Markers**: teal for ready, grey for checking, amber ring for needs your choice. Names appear from mid zoom.
- **Zoomed out**: markers group by country, with counts. **Zoomed in**: full names.
- **Click**: a side card with name, level, reason and "See replacements".
- **Disruption**: an orange ring at Rotterdam labelled "Strike at the port". Affected lines turn orange with one 600 ms pulse. The hub caption reads "3 suppliers are affected."

## 5. Risk and explanation
Each supplier shows the level word first ("High risk"), a coloured dot, then the score ("78 of 100"). Beneath, at most two reasons as full sentences: "2 of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." Colour never carries meaning alone. "How we know" lists sources and what could not be checked.

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
Today opens with one alert: "A strike at the Port of Rotterdam is hitting Nordic Steel. We noticed 4 minutes ago." Below, three ranked replacements as rows: name, city, badge ("Safe to approve", "Needs a check", "Ruled out") and one sentence why. Only the top safe option gets a filled button: "Approve Gdansk Metals." After approval: "Approved today by you", with Undo, logged in a short "Decisions" list.

## 7. Visual style
Light and calm. Background #F6F7F8, surface #FFFFFF, text #1C2430, secondary text #5B6675, dividers #E2E6EA. Brand teal #1F5F6B for the hub and primary buttons. Status: safe #2F7D57, check #A16207, ruled out #8A94A0, alert #C2410C. Inter throughout: 28px for the one headline, 16px body, 14px minimum labels. 8px spacing grid, 12px radius.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
Deeper tiers, ports and owners drawn on the map. Live activity logs and raw feeds. Confidence modes, IDs, provider names, status codes. Login, settings, charts, dark mode, export.
