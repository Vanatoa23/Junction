# e18: Traffic-light simplicity

## 1. Concept
Every supplier is a green, amber or red dot, and nothing else needs learning. A non-technical reader sees who is fine, who needs a look and who must be replaced within seconds.

## 2. Navigation and screens
Three screens: **Map** (home), **Suppliers**, **Alerts**. The home line states the situation in one sentence: "Two suppliers are affected by the Rotterdam closure." Top right: **Add supplier**. A small grey **Demo** button, bottom right, is for the presenter.

## 3. Add-supplier flow
A side panel asks only for **Supplier name**. Below, "Add more details (optional)" expands: website, country, city, what they supply, importance (Low, Medium, High), yearly spend, lead time, contact, notes. A "Paste a list" tab takes one name per line. The new card then shows "Found" and "Could not find" in plain lines.

## 4. Map
- Pale map, plain grey world outline offline.
- Hub: navy circle labelled with the company name, centred on Europe.
- Lines: thin, direct suppliers only, coloured by each dot.
- Dots: coloured by risk. A hollow "Checking" ring is the only other state, lasting seconds.
- Zoomed out: country counts. Zoomed in: names and cities.
- Click: side card with name, level, one reason, and "See options" if amber or red.
- Disruption: incident point pulses red once, then holds. Affected dots and lines turn red; hub caption: "Port of Rotterdam closed. 3 suppliers affected."

## 5. Risk and explanation
Shown as **Low**, **Medium** or **High**, always with its colour. The 0 to 100 number appears only in small text on the card. Reasons sit under "Why" as one to three plain sentences: "Two of its own suppliers ship through Rotterdam." Each card also states what could not be checked, so silence never looks like safety.

## 6. Alerts and alternatives
Alerts list newest first: dot, plain headline ("Port of Rotterdam closed by strike"), affected names, "Noticed 12 seconds after it was reported." Opening one shows up to three cards: **Safe to approve** (green) or **Needs a check** (amber). Ruled-out options hide behind "Show ruled out". Each card has one reason sentence and one **Approve** button. Approval turns it into "Approved by you, 14 Nov"; the new supplier joins the map in green.

## 7. Visual style
Light, for projection. Background #F6F5F1, surface #FFFFFF, text #1E2430, secondary text #5B6370, hub #1F3A5F, low #2E7D4F, medium #C97A10, high #B83227, lines #D8D4C8, land #E7E4DA, sea #D6E4EC. Inter: body 16px, labels 14px, headings 24px and 18px. 8px grid, 12px card radius, thin borders, no shadows. Colour never stands alone: every dot has its word.

## 8. What you deliberately leave out
Login, settings, editing beyond adding, tier-two suppliers, ports and owners drawn on the map, raw scores in lists, technical words or source names, dark mode, charts, tables, export, drag and drop.
